# Swaggy C trading strategy research

A detailed write-up of how forex and gold trader Chris "Swaggy C" Williams trades. It is built from the transcripts of every strategy-related video on his YouTube channel, scanned on 2026-10-22.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

| [ALL_VIDEOS_COMBINED.md](ALL_VIDEOS_COMBINED.md) | Every per-video write-up in one file, newest first. |
| [videos/](videos/) | One file per strategy video (45), plus [chart notes](videos/2026-visual-chart-notes.md) from a frame-by-frame review of his two April 2026 strategy videos. |

| [VIDEO_INDEX.md](VIDEO_INDEX.md) | All 116 full videos and livestreams, each marked CORE, PARTIAL or EXCLUDE with the reason. |

## How it was made

1. **Screening.** All 116 full videos and the livestream were screened. The first ~500 words of each transcript were read, then the rest was searched for strategy content. 27 videos are CORE (mainly strategy) and 17 are PARTIAL (strategy segments only). 69 were excluded as lifestyle, motivation, psychology, story or business content. One video uploaded on the scan date was added as PARTIAL.
2. **Write-ups.** Each included video's transcript was read in full and written up in the same 15-section format:
   - style and timeframes
   - tools
   - analysis steps
   - entries, stops, targets and management
   - risk
   - every trade walked through
   - rules
   - changes he states
   - gaps
   - quotes with timestamps
3. **Shorts.** 53 of the 319 Shorts were picked by title and reviewed.
4. **Charts.** The two newest strategy videos were reviewed frame by frame to capture what his charts show but he doesn't say.
5. **Master document.** Everything was combined into the master document, with each rule labelled STATED, INFERRED, CARRIED OVER or VISUAL and tied to a video and timestamp.

## Limits

- **Captions.** Transcripts are YouTube auto-captions. Misheard prices and pair names are corrected where obvious and marked [?] where not.
- **Chart details.** Many chart details are only pointed at in the videos ("this level right here"). In the write-ups these are marked "[shown on chart, not stated]". Only the two April 2026 videos were reviewed visually.
- **Undisclosed details.** He never states some things a 100% replica would need, such as:
  - the risk % per trade in the current system
  - the lot split across his limit-order ladder
  - his exact Fibonacci and EMA settings

  He sends viewers to his paid products for those. These gaps are listed in section 7 of the master document.
- **Missing transcripts.** Three videos returned empty transcripts, and judging by their titles none is about strategy.
- **What's included.** Full transcripts are not included, only short quotes.
