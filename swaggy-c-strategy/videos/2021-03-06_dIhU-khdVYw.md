## 1. What this video covers
His dedicated candlestick video. It is less a pattern catalogue and more a set of personal rules for **where and how** he uses candles. He only reads candlestick patterns at a **psychological level, trend line or Fibonacci retracement**. He wants the pattern confirmed on a **higher timeframe (1H/2H/4H)**, then drops to the **15-minute for an entry** on structure or a second pattern. He trades **level to level** (e.g. 0.78 → 0.79) in the direction of the 1-2 week outlook. He also covers the body-to-wick ratio as a momentum read, stop placement with "breathing room", and using **price alerts** at every psychological level instead of watching charts. This reflects his early-2021 intraday/swing level-to-level approach. The first few minutes (open/high/low/close, wick vs body) are textbook basics.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- **Higher timeframe for confirmation:** a pattern on the **1H or 2H** "tells me exactly what price is going to do for the next few days". On the **4H or daily** it tells him "what price is going to do for the next week or week and a half". [13:18–14:19]
- **His working flow:** confirmation on the **2H or 4H** (sometimes 1H), then "scale down to a 15 minute time frame and see the structure being created". [29:32–30:32, 54:47]
- He won't act on a 15m pattern at a psych level alone: "it's easy to get faked out." [29:32]
- **Direction horizon:** he wants to know what price will do over the next 24h, 48h, week, two weeks or month, and then trades only in that direction. "Some trades I may hold for a week straight." [54:47–55:48]
- He says he's not someone who trades "every 10 minutes". He places the trade with stop and target and walks away. [53:41–54:47]
- He started trading in 2015, and early on was "obsessed" with 1m/5m/15m candles. [6:11]

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- **Psychological levels** are his core framework. He trades between whole-number levels (e.g. 0.77, 0.78, 0.79). [9:12, 33:34]
  - **"New set of 10 digits"** levels (1.70, 1.80, 1.90; 0.70, 0.80, 0.90) are "a lot stronger" than 1.76/1.77-type levels. Price will "stop… consolidate… move around" there. [34:35]
  - **Quarter levels** are every **250 pips** (e.g. 1.10000, 1.12500, 1.15000; 0.70, 0.72500…), and he also uses their **mid-levels** (e.g. AUDUSD 0.77500). [37:36–38:36]
- **Trend lines and Fibonacci retracement** (38.2/50/61.8) as the other "major areas". [9:12, 22:25]
- **Moving averages:** "the MA crossover" is a confluence ("chances are the MA has done crossed over"). Periods are not stated here. He references his previous MA video (8/14 EMA).
- **TradingView alerts** at every nearby psychological level. When price consolidates he draws a box and sets alerts about **10 pips above and below**. [49:39–52:41]
- **Favourite patterns:** he uses "the same 10 or 11" patterns only. Named in this video: bullish/bearish engulfing, morning star, evening star, hammer, shooting star, doji, tweezer top/bottom, marubozu, head and shoulders/inverse, plus a "momentum candle". [10:13, 12:16, 48:39]

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Mark accurate **psychological levels / support-resistance, trend lines (and Fib)**. "You can know all the candlesticks in the world… if you don't have the correct levels on… you will be faked out." [35:35]
2. Work out the **overall direction on the higher timeframe** (4H/daily, weekly), e.g. "from 1.76 to 1.80… 400 pips". Expect it to come in steps such as "up 100 pips, down 40, up 50, down 20". [15:19]
3. Ignore candles "in the middle of nowhere". If price is already mid-move between levels, wait for the next level. [9:12–11:13, 45:38–46:38]
4. Set **alerts** at the surrounding psychological levels and walk away. [49:39]
5. When price reaches a level, look for a **candlestick formation** there. Single, double or triple doesn't matter. Use the **2H/4H** (sometimes 1H) for the confirmation. [12:16, 29:32]
6. Check the candle's **body-to-wick ratio**. Little or no wick means strong momentum. [41:37–43:38]
7. Get a third confluence, e.g. an MA crossover. "I only need three confirmations." [12:16]
8. **Drop to the 15m** and look for **structure** (a lower high/higher low, or a break of the last lower high/higher low) and/or another candlestick formation for the entry. [30:32]
9. Enter, put the stop a few pips beyond the pattern, and target the **next psychological level**. [31:32–33:34]
10. If the level just consolidates, box it, set alerts ~10 pips beyond the box, and re-assess when it breaks. [50:40–52:41]

## 6. Entry rules
- Enter only when a candlestick formation prints **at a psychological level, trend line or Fib retracement**, combined with another confluence (e.g. an MA crossover). "That's enough of a confirmation for me to get in, period. I only need three confirmations." [12:16]
- "Any type of bullish candlestick formation at these levels, I'm getting in", as long as it is "strong enough and not on like a one minute time frame". [19:21]
- **React, don't anticipate.** Don't wait for a specific pattern (e.g. an evening star) to form at a level, because you may get a bearish engulfing instead and miss the move. [13:18]
- **Higher-TF pattern, then 15m entry.** Confirm on 2H/4H, then enter on the 15m on structure (a lower high for shorts) or a second pattern (e.g. a 15m bearish engulfing). [30:32]
- **In-trend re-entries:** pullbacks to a psych/mid-level or a Fib 38.2/50/61.8 within the bigger move are counter-trends to buy or sell into. Example: a morning star at the 50% retracement means "we're looking to get back in". [16:20–17:20, 22:25–23:25]
- **Aggressive vs conservative:** enter on the confirmation candle right after the pattern (with a stop that has breathing room), or wait for price to **break the pattern's low/high (break, retest, continuation)**. [40:37–41:37]
- **Missed entries:** don't chase (no FOMO, e.g. after a friend says GBPUSD dropped 100 pips). Wait for price to create more structure, such as a lower high plus a bearish pattern, then a break. [45:38–48:39]
- **Scaling in:** "every single retracement, scale in, scale in… partial profit, get in, get out", in line with the trend found on the 4H. [55:48]

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Put the stop **beyond where the pattern printed**: below it for longs, above it for shorts. (He misspeaks "below an uptrend… above… on an uptrend".) [32:33]
- AUDUSD example: "your stop loss is a few pips above here, or even above here to be safe", i.e. above the 15m entry structure or above the 4H tweezer top. [28:29, 31:32]
- **No 2-5 pip stops:** "do not have a two pip stop loss or a five pip stop loss." "You can't have a five pip stop loss and then get wicked out every time… on GBPJPY, be more realistic." [31:32, 40:37]
- He mocks the idea that "if you have a stop loss over 10 pips you don't know how to trade". [56:49]
- Expect drawdown after entry: "there's no such thing as an absolute perfect entry… we have to anticipate some form of drawdown." [39:36]

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- **Level to level:** "All I do is trade the levels", e.g. 0.78 → 0.79. He won't assume a 300-pip run. [33:34]
- If price is still strong as it approaches the next level, he expects it to continue to the next level (e.g. "maybe that 500 level or the 1.80 level"). [33:34–34:35]
- After a TP, go back to the 4H and read the next candle at the level, e.g. a shooting star with no lower wick at 0.79 means "it's going to break past this .79 level and at least get to 0.78". [28:29–29:32]
- Fixed R:R, partial percentages: not specified (see §10).

## 9. Trade management
- **Alerts instead of screen-watching.** Alerts sit at every psych level. If consolidation follows, box it and set alerts about 10 pips above and below. When an alert fires he checks the candles. [49:39–52:41]
- "I'm placing my trade, I'm putting my stop loss and take profit and I'm walking away." [53:41]
- **Hold through retracements.** A pullback to 38.2/50/61.8 inside the move is "simply retracing… we're not looking to get out or get scared". [22:25]
- **Scale in on retracements and take partials**, in the direction of the 4H trend. [55:48]
- Some trades are held "for a week straight". [55:48]
- Break-even, trailing and weekend rules: not covered.

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- He says stop loss, take profit, R:R and **lot size** deserve "the same level of importance" as entries. He warns about **over-leveraging**, "a way bigger lot size than you should have", and blowing the account. [55:48–57:49]
- The R:R remark is garbled in the captions: "your risk to reward ratio isn't 1 to 3 every single time, so that means you have to win you know 80% of your trades". He seems to be saying that a poor R:R forces a very high win rate, which implies he favours about **1:3** [?]. [56:49]
- Small accounts ($300/$500) are no excuse to trade low timeframes. Still analyse on the 1H+ to avoid being wicked out. [59:50]
- No risk % or lot numbers are given.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Don't read every low-timeframe candle: "do not get caught up in every single candlestick." [8:12]
- Only read candles at major areas: "I'm only looking for one thing… what it does on a major area, a psychological level, a trend line or fibonacci retracement." [9:12]
- No mid-range entries: "If price is in the middle of those levels and it's already moving… I'm not looking to enter." [9:12]
- Candles need support/resistance: "Candlestick patterns are a main confluence with me but… they're nothing without support and resistance." [11:13]
- Three confirmations are enough: "I only need three confirmations for me to get in." [12:16]
- Don't anticipate a pattern: "you can't anticipate that… all you can do is react to the market." [13:18]
- Confirm on the higher timeframe: "I need that confirmation on a two hour or a four hour time frame, once I get that then I can scale down to a 15 minute." [29:32–30:32]
- Trade level to level: "all I do is trade the levels." [33:34]
- Respect round numbers: levels like 1.70/1.80 are "a lot stronger" than 1.76/1.77. [34:35]
- Give the stop room: "do not have a two pip stop loss or a five pip stop loss." [31:32]
- Read the wick: "the less of a wick you see, the more confirmation that it's going to go up." [42:37]
- Don't chase: "you got in when the move was already created." [47:38]
- Use alerts: "a way I can combat this is by setting alerts at every single psychological level around me." [49:39]
- Avoid news: don't trade around fundamental announcements. [18:20]
- Don't over-leverage: watch your lot size and R:R as closely as your entries. [56:49]
