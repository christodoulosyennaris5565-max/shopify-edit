## 1. What this video covers
He teaches why you must wait for candle closes — on the right timeframe and at a key level — before acting, using GBPUSD (and GBPAUD) examples. Key points: plot key levels and mid-levels first; a wick through a level is not a break; candles with far more wick than body signal reversal or consolidation; the higher the timeframe the candle closes on, the stronger the signal. He also shows his 2020 breakout rules with the two moving averages (crossover timing vs consolidation break), the take-profit/break-even decision when price stalls short of target, the "three legs" market structure idea, and a Fibonacci 61.8 rejection + bearish engulfing to the 27 extension. This is his 2020 intraday/swing level-to-level method.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- Trades level to level ("I trade levels") [10:19].
- 4H = structure: "the four hours where I look at pure structure" [13:19].
- 1H = entries ("one hour is more for my entries, same as the 15 minute") [13:19]; he notes he doesn't "even get the confirmations" on the 1H [?].
- Wait for the 1H close rather than acting on 15m/5m candles [5:15]-[6:16].
- Daily and 4H closes matter most; daily close below 1.80 on GA used for bias [18:24]-[19:26].

## 3. Markets, pairs and sessions
- "I only trade these three pairs anyway": GU (GBPUSD), GA (GBPAUD), and gold [3:14].
- Master one pair: knowing key levels "by heart" [3:14]-[4:14]; "stick to one pair, one or two pairs and just master them" [19:26]-[20:26].
- Session context matters: is GBPUSD consolidating because it's the Asian session, because London just opened, or because London is closing as New York arrives? [6:16]
- Brokers "pushing price" to stop people out — levels where price was pushed to are significant [15:20]-[16:21].

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- Key levels plus a **mid-level** between each pair of key levels (e.g. 1.24500 between 1.24 and 1.25 [?]) [4:14].
- Advanced traders keep only 1–3 levels on the chart [3:14], [17:21].
- Two moving averages, blue and red; "blue above red" etc.; MA = moving support/resistance/trendline [7:16], [9:19]. Periods not stated.
- Fibonacci: A-B-C; four levels "it moves in one of these four levels" [?]; 61.8 retracement entry; 27 extension target; 50 level mentioned [17:21], [19:26].
- Candlestick patterns: doji, shooting star, pin bar formation (3 candles), bearish engulfing, evening star.
- Bar replay.

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Plot key levels (and mid-levels) before anything else [3:14].
2. Identify the 4H trend/structure; trade with it [7:16], [13:19].
3. Note the session/time of day [6:16].
4. Watch for consolidation: MAs tangled together = no clear trend; mark the consolidation box [8:17].
5. Wait for a candle to **close** beyond the consolidation/level on the 1H (wicks don't count) [8:17]-[9:19].
6. Check MA crossover timing relative to the break, and whether price respects the MA as resistance/support [9:19]-[10:19].
7. Enter on the break/retest with candlestick confirmation; target the next level [10:19].
8. Use Fib (61.8 rejection) for re-entries during retracements; target 27 extension [17:21]-[18:24].

## 6. Entry rules
- Wait for the hourly close; don't buy off a big 15m/5m candle into a level with repeated wicks [5:15]-[6:16].
- Breakout + MA crossover: three scenarios learned from his mentor — (a) break and MA cross at the same time; (b) break first, cross later; (c) MAs cross inside the consolidation before the break ("more aggressive") [9:19]. After closure below consolidation, he waits for "confirmation" because the MAs crossed [9:19].
- Retest of the MA / box after the break, then continue [10:19].
- Strong 4H shooting star bouncing off MA resistance: "you better sell" [13:19].
- Example entry after a 3-candle pin bar formation where the third candle breaks below the MA and red crosses above blue at the same time; plus a bearish engulfing that rejects the Fib 61.8 [16:21]-[18:24].
- Re-enter after a retrace only on clear confirmation [11:19], [14:20].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- On a sell after a break: "stop-loss above here" (above the broken consolidation / recent high) [14:20] [shown on chart, not stated].
- Long example: "stop-loss right below the last wick" [5:15].
- No pip size stated.

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Next key level: e.g. GBPUSD 1.23000 [?] after the 1.24500 level [10:19]; with strong 4H shooting star, go to "the next level" (1.22) [13:19].
- Fib 27 extension [18:24]; GA target 1.77500 = the 61.8 level [?] [19:26].

## 9. Trade management
- If price doesn't reach the target, two options [10:19]-[11:19]:
  1. Move stop to break-even and let it hit the target eventually (relaxed approach).
  2. Take profit early; "you can never get mad at taking profit" (mentor's advice); re-enter on clear confirmation after the retrace.
- Get out at the 27 extension when candles start shrinking/closing back up: "that tells you 27 is done" [20:26].
- On GA: if a doji appears near target and you're hesitating, get out [19:26].

## 11. Trades walked through in this video

### Trade 1: GBPUSD long (losing example) — premature 15m entry (5:15–8:17)
- Context: price at the 1.24500 mid-level [?] between key levels. On the 15m/5m a big bullish candle pushes up after retesting the MA; instinct is to buy, stop below last wick, TP above.
- Result: immediately negative, loses. Reason: didn't wait for the 1H close — 1H had four wicks in a row at 1.24500, body smaller than wick, MAs never crossed (blue not above red) on 1H or 4H; downtrend intact.

### Trade 2: GBPUSD short — consolidation break (8:17–11:19)
- 1H consolidation with MAs together; candles more wick than body (body ~10% of wick). A wick below support doesn't count. Then a close below; MAs crossed over inside the consolidation; clear separation; retest of MA/box; down.
- Target 1.23000 [?]; 1.24500 mid-level for partials [?]. Price didn't reach 1.23 → break-even or take profit early.

### Trade 3: GBPUSD — market-structure outlook (11:19–13:19)
- Price moves in three legs (leg, retracement/consolidation, leg...); expects a fourth leg down. Counter-trend line being broken is just retracement; no HH/HL. Prediction: GBPUSD pushes further down that week.

### Trade 4: GBPUSD short — 4H shooting star (13:19–14:20)
- 4H shooting star closing off MA resistance: sell, target beyond 1.23 to 1.22. Doji clusters on the 4H; entries "right here, stop-loss above here" on the 1H breakdown — "you're never negative".

### Trade 5: GBPUSD short — retest + pin bar + Fib 61.8 (14:20–18:24)
- After retrace: 7 dojis out of last 13–14 1H candles = indecision. Price retests to a tee the consolidation break level (where brokers stopped people out). Pin bar formation (3 candles), third breaks below MA; red crosses above blue at the same time.
- Fib A (top) to B (bottom): exact 61.8 retracement; bearish engulfing close rejects the 61.8. Exit at the 27 extension. Result: target hit.

### Trade 6: GBPAUD short — outlook (18:24–20:26)
- Daily closed below 1.80; shooting stars off the MA and off 1.80 [?] key level. Very bearish. Possible retrace to a Fib 50 level, then drop to 1.77500 = 61.8 level [?] (skipping the 27 extension because he knows how GA moves). Exit if a doji appears and he hesitates.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Plot key levels first: "The first thing I would do is make sure my key levels are on here" [3:14].
- Wicks aren't breaks: "If price wicks below a support but it does not close below, that support still held firm. Period." [8:17]
- Beware wicky candles: "Whenever you see a situation where there's way more wick than body, be very careful" [7:16].
- Trade with the trend: "You got to go with the actual overall trend" [7:16].
- Higher-timeframe closes count more: "The higher the timeframe the candle closes on, the more aggressive you should be" [13:19]-[14:20].
- Take profits without regret: "You can never get mad at taking profit. Period." [11:19]
- Keep charts clean: "I try to keep it clear so my mind can look at it in its purest form" [17:21].
- Master pairs: "Stick to one pair, one or two pairs and just master them" [19:26].
