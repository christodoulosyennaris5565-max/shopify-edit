## 1. What this video covers
Despite the title, this is a bar-replay walkthrough of his price-action strategy (the backtesting is shown by replaying a GBPAUD 4-hour/1-hour chart). He says to trade only a few pairs — his non-negotiables are gold, GBPAUD and GBPUSD, plus AUDUSD (swappable with EURUSD) — and to know each pair's key levels and fundamental drivers. Key levels are round numbers ("where the banks and government like their levels"), drawn as boxes. He trades consolidation at levels, mini evening stars, dojis and confirmation candles that break the MA, targets the next round number or Fib -27 extension, and adjusts targets to structure. This is the early-2020 version.

- Key levels at round numbers (banks/government prefer 1.900, 1.850, not 1.83225) [2:02].
- GBPAUD levels: 1.9000, 1.9250 (mid), 1.9500, 1.8750 (mid), 1.8500 [2:02, 8:05].

- Moving average — break below/above as confirmation. Period not stated.

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Limit yourself to a few pairs and learn their levels and drivers [0:02].
2. On a blank 4-hour chart, mark only the round-number key levels near price, box them [2:02].
3. When price reaches the level, draw a consolidation box; read direction from small candles not breaking the MA [3:03].
4. Scale to 15m and wait for breakout of a mini consolidation in the expected direction [3:03–4:03].
5. Enter; stop above the area; TP next level [4:03].
6. After a break, expect a retest of the box ("it will come back to be tested always") [5:04].
7. Look for mini evening star + confirmation candle below MA for the next short [5:04–6:04].
8. Adjust target to structure (last place price launched from) [8:05].
9. Delete used boxes; keep chart clean [11:08].

## 6. Entry rules
- At a level, sell only if consolidation with "little candlesticks" isn't breaking above the MA [3:03].
- Breakout of mini-consolidation in the expected direction [4:03].
- Retest of box + mini evening star + confirmation candle breaking below MA [5:04–6:04].
- Further shorts only after "a full break below" a level, judged by candle body close (not wick) [6:04, 9:06].
- Long: three touches of a low that won't break + doji + confirmation candle; or doji at a Fib 61.8 rejection breaking above MA [10:07, 14:09].
- Long after five rejections of a box low [16:11].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Above the consolidation area (shorts) [4:03].
- "Above the last high if it's a downtrend, or below the last low in an uptrend, to give you the most accurate, reasonable room" [7:04].
- Above-level move invalidates the trade [5:04].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Next round-number level (e.g. 1.9000) [3:03, 6:04].
- Don't over-extend: keep 1.90 as TP until it fully breaks [6:04].
- Adjust: target 1.8750 moved ~40 pips to the last area price launched from [8:05].
- Fib -27 extension ("1.27") [12:08, 13:08]. Long TP 1.8950 [11:08].

## 9. Trade management
- Exit at target; reassess [9:06, 12:08].
- Exit early if price shows it won't reach target [8:05].
- Keep weekly levels; delete mini levels/boxes to keep chart clean [9:06, 11:08].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- "Try to only keep your pairs to a few" [0:02].
- Know your pairs: "you got to understand what fundamental announcements will push that pair" [0:02].
- Round numbers: banks prefer "1.900… as opposed to 1.83225" [2:02].
- Don't clutter levels: "you're gonna have a cluttered chart… price is not gonna respect any of these levels" [1:02–2:02].
- Retests: "whenever price breaks through a level of consolidation… it will come back to be tested always" [5:04].
- Body closes: "the wick is just where it went… the bodies are where it closes" [6:04].
- Execute on 15m: "any time I'm about to trade… I scale down to a 15-minute time frame" [7:04].
- Keep chart clean; delete old boxes [11:08].
- Pure price action: "there's no indicator except moving average… no fancy scanner" [12:08].
- React to signals: "if it gives me a doji and it's supposed to go up… I'm buying; if it gives me an evening star… I'm selling" [17:11].
