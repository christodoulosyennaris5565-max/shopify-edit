## 1. What this video covers
A market breakdown of gold at all-time highs (~$1930-1950), DXY, AUDUSD, GBPUSD and GBPAUD, used to teach several rules: match the timeframes you look at (and the closes you wait for) to your trader type; gold's large pip ranges and why small accounts should avoid it; cloning a consolidation range to project moves; and his "moving average bounce" entry (enter when price bounces off/rejects the MA, stop just below). It reflects his 2020 intraday/swing level-to-level approach.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- "I'm an intraday slash swing trader. I don't really scalp" — day trades from day to day and also holds swing trades [7:07].
- Timeframes by trader type [7:07], [17:15]-[18:15]:
  - Scalper: should not look for weekly closes or weekly/monthly charts.
  - Intraday trader (holds ~4-5 hours): glance at the weekly at most; really focus on 4H and 2H, "daily at the highest", plus 1H; enter on the 15-minute.

- Analyzes top-down "start of the daily and you analyze down" to the 2H for intraday [12:11].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Decide what type of trader you are and look only at the matching timeframes [7:07].
2. Check higher-timeframe context (for gold: monthly all-time high; DXY weekly close below support) [3:03], [6:06].
3. Mark the current consolidation range (gold 1930–1945/1950) and clone it to see where the next leg could go [8:07].
4. On the trade pair, go to the daily and analyze down to the 2H: MAs pointing up, price trending up [12:11].
5. Look for price pulling back to and bouncing off the moving average, with extra confluence (marubozu/bullish engulfing above support) [12:11]-[14:12].
6. Enter on the bounce, stop below it, first TP at the most recent high / next level [13:11].

## 6. Entry rules
- MA bounce: "Whenever price bounces off a moving average, enter, enter, enter... because chances are price is going to continue" [12:11]; "If price bounces off a moving average and rejects it, enter" [14:12].
- Confluence usually present: bounce + a big marubozu or bullish engulfing + above a support level [14:12].
- MA is different from S/R — price always retests S/R, but if it bounces off an MA and can't break it, that's a signal [12:11]-[13:11].
- Don't wait for a box break when there's room to the target: an AU 100-110 pip move — "you can find an entry in between there" [12:11].
- A wick through support or an MA is not a break; the body must close beyond it [18:15].
- Don't second-guess: "enter, don't second-guess yourself" [15:13].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- MA bounce: "your stop loss is right below here" — below the bounce candle/low; "very tight stop loss" [13:11].
- Gold stop sizing: on gold $10 is 100 pips (1935 to 1925 = 100 pips, not 10); ask "can you afford to have that 100 pip stop loss" [9:08]-[10:09].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Match timeframes to style: "If you are a scalper you should not be looking for weekly closes" [7:07].
- Intraday: "you're better off staying on a daily at the highest, but the four hour, two hour and even the one hour... and enter on the 15 minute" [17:15].
- MA bounce entries: "If price bounces off a moving average and rejects it, enter" [14:12].
- "A moving average is a moving trend line... moving support, moving resistance" [14:12].
- Wicks don't break levels: "Just because a wick goes below support... doesn't mean it broke. The body is still above" [18:15].
- No gold for beginners: "Do not trade gold if you are a new trader or you've only been trading for a few months" [9:08].
- Small accounts: "If you have a small account don't be trying to trade gold, focus on the majors" [15:13].
- Trendline breaks: "if price has a trend line... and it breaks it, it's gonna touch all the points" [19:15].
