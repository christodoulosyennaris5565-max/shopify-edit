## 1. What this video covers
A vlog episode (Christmas, new studio, Mexico trip, merch, a possible move to Texas). The trading content is a four-minute segment, apparently filmed around late December 2020 / January 2021, where he says he is **thinking about changing his overall strategy**: instead of being in the market every day and closing every Friday, he wants to place a few trades in the direction of a longer-term target and **hold them for one to three weeks**, because banks and funds work toward monthly and quarterly goals. He would keep intraday trading on volatile pairs (GBPAUD, GBPJPY) and hold longer on majors (AUDUSD, EURUSD, USDCAD) and pairs tied to precious metals. He uses AUDUSD 0.70 → 0.78 and GBPUSD 1.28 → his 1.40 target as examples. He stresses this is a direction he's heading in, "not my overall strategy fully right now."

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
**Before (his "usual strategy"):**
- Intraday + swing; never a scalper. "I've always swung for four days, a week, a week and a half" [5:05] — but in practice closing every Friday and re-entering Sunday/Monday toward the same target [3:04–4:04].

**What he noticed (self-audit):**
- "I've actually audited myself and caught myself placing only one or two trades a week but having a 15 lot size or 8 lot size or 20 lot size, even a 10, and just letting it go as high as it possibly can or as low as it possibly can" [2:04].

**New direction (being backtested "in the last few weeks"):**
- "Get in the trade, place a few trades and just hold them for two weeks, three weeks" [3:04]; also "one week" [4:04].
- Still "going to be an intraday trader and be in the market here and there" [2:04], and some pairs must stay intraday (hold 1–3 days) [4:04].
- He accepts the weekend gap risk: "I know I'm going to be subjected to a little bit of risk when the market is closed on Saturdays and when it opens back up" [3:04].
- Timeframes: not stated in this segment.

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Form a longer-term directional target from fundamentals and levels (e.g. AUDUSD to 0.75; GBPUSD to 1.40 if the UK leaves the EU without further delay) [3:04–4:04].
2. Think in institutional time frames: banks and funds "have a common goal to reach at the end of the month, at the end of the quarter" (Q1–Q4); FOMC meets eight times a year; NFP is monthly [2:04].
3. Instead of trading the daily fluctuations, place "maybe three or four trades" in that direction and hold them toward the target for 1–3 weeks [3:04].
4. Choose the pair type: majors/metal-linked pairs for holding; volatile GBP crosses for intraday only [4:04].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Never scalp: "I'm not a scalper whatsoever ... it works for some people, never worked for me" [5:05].
- Think like institutions: "Every bank and institution has a common goal for Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4" [5:05].
