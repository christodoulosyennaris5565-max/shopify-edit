## 1. What this video covers
His top-down timeframe workflow in 2020: daily (sometimes weekly, only the last 2–3 weeks) for bias, 4-hour and 1-hour for confirmation and levels, then the 5- or 15-minute for a "sniper" entry with a tight stop, targeting the next key level. He walks through a GBPAUD short (claimed 1:10), a GBPAUD long reversal from a level (1:4.5), and a EURUSD long off a 4-hour hammer with a 5-minute entry (around 1:17–1:23). He stresses that MAs are short-period (not 50/100), that anything above 1:3 is good, and that getting stopped out on a tight stop is fine — just re-enter. This is the 2020 level-to-level intraday/swing version.

- He re-assesses every time price reaches a new level (trades back and forth level to level) [5:09, 7:10].
- Holding period not stated explicitly; trades go from one level to the next.

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- Moving averages — "relatively close" short periods, "not a 50 or 100 EMA"; exact periods given in his separate MA video, not stated here [2:05]. MA crossover is his entry trigger; MA also acts as "a moving support or resistance" [5:09].
- If price is "in the middle of the moving average" (MAs tangled/price chopping through them) it is still consolidating — don't trade [9:12].
- Horizontal key levels; trades "level to level and mid level" [3:05].
- Consolidation boxes [4:07, 6:09].
- Candlestick patterns: bearish engulfing, bullish engulfing, hammer, Marubozu ("mr. disclose"), shooting star mentioned [4:07, 6:09, 8:10].
- Market structure (higher highs/lows, lower highs/lows).

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Start on the daily: check MA direction and how many consecutive days price has moved one way (e.g. 13 straight bearish days on GBPAUD = sell-only bias) [2:05–3:05].
2. Optionally glance at weekly — but only the last 2–3 weeks matter [7:10, 12:16].
3. 4-hour: confirm same structure (lower highs/lower lows) and mark the levels you will trade between [3:05].
4. 1-hour: same, for confirmation [3:05].
5. If price is sitting at a level and the daily trend is choppy (e.g. 6–7 days down, 6 days up), hold no bias and wait for a candlestick signal at the level (e.g. 4-hour hammer) [7:10–8:10].
6. Scale down to 5- or 15-minute; draw a box around the consolidation; wait for a break of the box, retest, MA crossover, and a lower high (shorts) / higher low (longs) [4:07, 9:12].
7. Enter, stop at the nearest structure, take profit at the next level seen on the 4-hour [4:07–5:09].
8. When price reaches the level, exit and reassess for the next trade (which may be the opposite direction) [5:09–6:09].

## 6. Entry rules
- Direction must agree with higher-timeframe bias; with a sell bias "I'm only looking for sells" [3:05].
- Shorts on the 5-minute: price breaks the support box, retests it, comes back down → entry; confluences: MA crossover, bearish engulfing, Marubozu close, lower high/lower low [4:07].
- Patient alternative: wait for the next MA crossover + another lower high [4:07].
- Longs after 4-hour bullish engulfing: 1-hour MA crossover, break above the box, higher low, retest of MA that can't break → entry [6:09].
- Reversal at a level: 4-hour hammer at the end of a downtrend → mark it on the 1-hour (1-hour MA crossover) → scale to 5-minute, only looking for buys → wait for break of resistance + retest + MA crossover + price trading above the MAs (not inside them) → enter long [8:10–9:12].
- "Price has to break a level, retest it and continue" [9:12].
- Order type not stated (entries on candle close implied).

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Place the stop so it's tight, at the structure that defines the entry: above the lower high for shorts; below the higher low / below the candle that tested the MA / below the box for longs [4:07, 6:09–7:10, 10:13].
- "You can't put a stop loss 5 pips below your trade, you'll get stopped out every time" — it must be at a real structure point (e.g. the higher low that bounced off the MA) [12:16–13:16].
- If a tight stop gets hit, "get back in" with a similar stop [12:16–13:16].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- "Your take profit is the next level" [4:07]; "go back to a 4-hour timeframe" to see it [4:07–5:09].
- R:R: "always 1:3, 1:4, 1:5", sometimes "1:12, 1:15, 1:20" [3:05]; "anything over 1:3 in my eyes is good" [7:10].
- Exit at the level: "the minute price gets to a level I'm out the trade" [5:09].

## 9. Trade management
- On reaching the level, close and reassess; look for the next trade (e.g. after a short hits the level, a 4-hour bullish engulfing gives a long) [5:09–6:09].
- Pullbacks that respect the MA are normal; MA = moving support/resistance [5:09].
- Warns against closing early out of emotion when a big wick appears; let it run to the level [11:15].
- No break-even/partials discussed here.

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- R:R minimum ~1:3; examples 1:10, 1:4.5, ~1:17–1:23 [3:05, 5:09, 7:10, 11:15].
- Losses accepted: "I got stopped out for a hundred or 200 dollars, if I end up with $5,000 that day I won" [13:16]. "You can shoot 9 for 23 as long as you get the win at the end of the day" [13:16].
- Says he made about $4,000 on the GBPAUD sell "last week" [2:05].
- No risk % or lot sizes stated. Mentions a future video on building a trading plan [12:16].

- Stop: above the lower high [shown on chart].

### Trade 3: EURUSD long (7:10–11:15)

- Stop: very tight below the MA retest / higher low [shown on chart].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Recent price action over long-term trend: "I pay attention to the most recent price action, which is why my moving averages are where they are" [2:05].
- "I'm only looking for sells" when daily/4h/1h are all bearish [3:05].
- Get in low-timeframe, plan on high-timeframe: "get in on a lower time frame based on a higher time frame" [3:05].
- "Anything over 1 to 3 in my eyes is good" [7:10].
- "The minute price gets to a level I'm out the trade and I try to reassess" [5:09].
- "Anytime price gets to a level I am stopping and analyzing" [7:10].
- Don't trade when price is inside the MAs: "it's still consolidating, I wouldn't trade it" [9:12].
- Don't put meaningless tight stops: "you can't put a stop loss 5 pips below your trade" [12:16].
- If stopped out, re-enter: "if it were to stop me out, guess what, I'd get right back in with a similar stop-loss" [13:16].
- Follow your trading plan: "as long as you are following your rules you are fine" [12:16].
