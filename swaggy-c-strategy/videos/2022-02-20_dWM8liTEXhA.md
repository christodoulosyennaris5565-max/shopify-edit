## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Hold a higher-timeframe directional bias (GBPAUD down) [3:02].
2. Mark the level below current price and the last high (the potential lower high) [2:02].
3. Wait for a legitimate candle close below the level, with price not taking out the last high → confirms "a great lower high" [2:02].
4. Enter; stop a few pips above the last high; take profit at a fixed level [2:02–3:02].
5. If price instead reverses up, switch to trading the bullish move — be flexible [3:02].

## 6. Entry rules

- The close must come without price taking out the previous high (the lower high stays intact): "if price breaks below here and does not take this high out" [2:02].
- Order type: not stated (reads as market entry after the close).

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Wait for a real close: "not a break below ... but actual legitimate close below here" [2:02].
- Stop above the lower high: "my stop loss, just maybe a few pips above that last [high]" [2:02].
- Trust your analysis: "doesn't matter if you're right or wrong, just trust your analysis" [3:02].
- Be flexible: "I don't care if you are right in your analysis as long as you are right when it comes to the actual trade" [3:02].
- Don't dismiss small profits: "do not discriminate on the type of profits" [4:03].
- No over-leveraging; judge success by % growth with good R:R [17:29].
