# Swaggy C: visual chart notes from the two newest strategy videos (April 2026)

This is a frame-by-frame visual review of what is on his TradingView screen. The transcripts miss this. The source is 480p video (742x480). Frames were upscaled and cropped to read the labels. The working frames are in the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/vid/frames/`) and are **not** copied into the repo. Their names are given as `787_<sec>.png` / `ouz_<sec>.png`.

How to read the numbers below:
- **Read** means the value was legible on screen.
- **Derived** means the label was too blurry to read. The value was computed from the Fibonacci levels that could be read. Every derived value matched every legible label it could be checked against.
- **[unreadable at 480p]** means the value could not be read or derived.

Platform: TradingView, light theme. Broker feed: OANDA for USDJPY and XAUUSD, FOREXCOM for GBPAUD. Watchlist: XAUUSD, BTCUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, GBPAUD, 6J1! (yen futures).

### Where the visuals clarify or contradict the audio (video 1)
- **"8 and 14 EMA"**: the chart always shows exactly **two thin MAs**. The faster one is **blue/blue-grey** and the slower one is **red/salmon**. The indicator legend is hidden, so the type and period cannot be confirmed on screen [unreadable at 480p]. The settings are spoken only.
- **Psych levels are not exact**: he says to type 160.000 exactly, but the 155, 150 and 145 lines sit at 155.080, 150.154 and 145.046. The purple rectangle absorbs the error.
- **Fib is redrawn on the latest swing**: the first Fib runs 146.409 → 159.425. The working Fib runs 146.662 → 159.458 (Jan 2026 high). The replay lesson uses the second one. It was drawn with hindsight: the 0% is a high that formed later than some of the replayed bars.
- **"Stop in the middle between 78.6 and 100"**: on screen the USDJPY stop is 148.518, which is between the two levels but closer to 78.6. The GBPAUD stop is also between 78.6 and 100.
- **"Move stop into profit … 400 pips in profit"**: on screen he drags the stop to **break-even (entry, 0.001 away)**, not 400 pips above it. The 400 pips refers to open profit.
- **Fixed $ risk**: every position tool shows **"Amount: 750"** (USDJPY, GBPAUD, and also USDCAD in video 2). That is consistent with risking a fixed $750 per trade. He never says it.
- **TP ladder from the audio, shown as Fib levels**: TP1 0%, TP2 -27%, TP3 -61.8%, TP4 = next big psych number (170.048 on screen).

## What the charts show about his current method (April 2026)

- **Timeframes**: analysis on **Daily and Weekly**. Weekly gives the 3-month bias and the big Fib. Daily is used to place the Fib, zones and limit orders. The **15m** appears only to show intraday noise or to zoom into an open trade. No 1m/5m/1h/4h setups are shown.
- **Instruments shown**: USDJPY (main), GBPAUD (short), USDCAD (long), XAUUSD (levels only).
- **Indicators**: two thin moving averages, fast = blue/blue-grey and slow = red/salmon. He says they are the 8 and 14 EMA; the legend is hidden, so the type and period are [unreadable at 480p]. No oscillators or volume.
- **Level drawing**:
  - Horizontal black lines at **round psychological numbers**: every 5.00 on USDJPY (160/155/150/145); every 0.025 on GBPAUD (2.050, 2.025, 2.000, 1.975, …); 1.400 / 1.375 on USDCAD; every $100 (and some $50) on gold.
  - **Thin purple rectangles** (±~0.2–0.3 JPY, ~±20 pips on GBPAUD) wrap the main lines as zones.
  - A thin box outlined in light blue/black marks prior swing highs/lows that flipped.
  - A big **beige rectangle** marks a base/range.
  - A **yellow box** marks the swing low that anchors the Fib.
- **Trendlines**: thick blue main trendline plus thinner "inner/outer" lines. Longs use a rising line from the major lows; shorts use a falling line from the major highs. The **third touch** is used as an entry confluence.
- **Fibonacci settings** (identical on every chart): **-61.8, -27, 0, 23.6, 38.2, 50, 61.8, 78.6, 100**. Labels show percent and price, on the right of the drawing. Colours: 0/23.6/100 grey, 38.2/50 red, 61.8 green, 78.6 light blue, extensions yellow/orange. The tool shows no 0.705/0.886/1.618/-1.0.
  - **Anchoring**: longs go **swing low (100%) → swing high (0%)**; shorts go **swing high (100%) → swing low (0%)**. The extensions are therefore beyond the 0% in the trade direction. He anchors on the latest completed impulse on the Daily/Weekly (USDJPY Sep-2025 low → Jan-2026 high; GBPAUD 2.015 → 1.871; USDCAD 1.367 → 1.397).
- **Entries**: **limit orders spread across a purple box covering 38.2–61.8** (USDJPY replay), or the 50–61.8 box on USDCAD. The biggest lot size goes at the deepest level. Actual position-tool entries: USDJPY 152.835 (between 50 and 61.8); GBPAUD ≈ 61.8; USDCAD 1.37835 (= 61.8).
- **Stops**: always **beyond 78.6% and before 100%** (USDJPY 148.518; USDCAD 1.37091; GBPAUD between 1.984 and 2.015). Widths shown: 431.7 pips (USDJPY W), 327 pips (GBPAUD W), 74.4 pips (USDCAD D). After price moves, the stop goes to **break-even** (shown on screen).
- **Targets**:
  - The first TP is **0%** (prior high).
  - The others are the **-27%** and **-61.8%** extensions, then the **next big psych number** (USDJPY 170.048).
  - The USDCAD single target, 1.40752, sits between -27 and -61.8.
- **R:R shown**: USDJPY **≈3.4** (TP at -61.8%) → **≈4** (TP 170); GBPAUD **≈5.6**; USDCAD **3.92**. **Fixed risk "Amount: 750"** on every position tool.
- **Directional filter**: every example trades in the direction of his 3-month projection: USDJPY up to 163–167 (cursor on Jul 10 2026), USDCAD up to 1.405–1.415, GBPAUD down to 1.78. His 3-month projection targets are the Fib extensions.
