## 1. What this video covers
How he uses the Fibonacci retracement tool in 2020: never on its own, always in confluence with support/resistance, trend lines and candlestick patterns. He draws it on a significant impulse (top-to-bottom in a downtrend, bottom-to-top in an uptrend), waits for price to react at 38.2/50/61.8/78.6 with a clear candle, then drops to the 15- or 5-minute for the entry. Targets are the -27 extension (first) and -61.8 extension (second/scale-ins), as an intermediate target before the next key level. He lists his whole toolset: S/R key levels, trend lines, one MA, Fibonacci, Japanese candlestick patterns. This is the 2020 version; note the contrast with his 2025-26 use of Fib 50/61.8 limit orders.

- **Fibonacci settings he says to copy**: 0, 38.2, 50, 61.8, 78.6 (captions "70.6"/"seventy twenty six"/"seventeen point six"), 1 (100), and extensions **-27** and **-61.8** [5:09–6:09]. ("You can copy the prices, the levels, everything I have here.")
- Drawing: downtrend — from swing high (100 at top) to swing low (0 at bottom); uptrend — from swing low to swing high [5:09, 14:15].
- ABCD labelling: A = swing high, B = swing low (0), C = retracement reversal point, D = extension target [10:11].
- Counter-trend line drawn on the retracement for confluence [10:11].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Have key S/R levels on the chart first — needed to distinguish a **reversal** at a key level from a mere retracement [3:08–4:09].
2. Wait for a significant impulse move away from the level [4:09].
3. When price starts to pull back, draw the Fib (high→low in downtrend) [5:09].
4. Do not blindly enter at 38.2/50/61.8; wait for a significant/"overbearing" candle showing the reaction [5:09, 7:09].
5. Drop to 15m (then 5m) — draw a box around consolidation, wait for a box break or lower highs [7:09].
6. Enter; stop above the box (or tighter above a lower high for a sniper entry); target the -27 extension [8:09].
7. At target, exit; delete the Fib; reassess and redraw a new Fib if a new swing/pullback forms [9:09–10:11].
8. Add a counter-trend line on the pullback; when it breaks with lower highs and failure at the MA, enter/scale in [10:11–11:12].

## 6. Entry rules
- Fib is never traded alone: "Fibonacci is not to be traded by itself, it's meant to be… in confluence with support, resistance, the trend lines and major market patterns" [3:08].
- Enter on the pullback, never chase the impulse: "don't even try to enter, just wait for it to retrace" [15:15].
- Confirmation needed at the Fib level: a strong candle / candlestick pattern (doji, hammer, shooting star, evening/morning star) printing on 38.2/50/61.8/78.6 "is telling you to get in" [7:09, 16:17].
- On 15m: lower high + break of structure/box → short (mirror for longs) [7:09–8:09].
- Also: counter-trend line break + price failing at the MA + lower highs [11:12].
- Scale in on further retracements [12:13, 14:15].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Above the consolidation box, or tighter above the lower high for a sniper entry [8:09].
- Sniper entry on 5m/15m → very tight stop (example 1:26 R:R) versus a much wider stop if entering on higher timeframe (1:4) [11:12–12:13].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Normal take profit = next key level; the Fib extension gives an earlier target when the next level is far away ("no man's land") [1:06–2:06, 6:09].
- First target: **-27 extension**; if price goes beyond it, **-61.8 extension** [8:09–9:09].
- With multiple positions: first trade TP at -27, scale-ins at -61.8 [12:13].
- If the next support is very close, target it instead [11:12].
- Sometimes price stops at 0 (the prior low) but more often reaches the extension [9:09].

## 9. Trade management
- At target, close: "the minute it does that, get out the trade" [8:09–9:09].
- Once in, don't micromanage — set stop properly and let it play out; closing on consolidation made you miss the move [13:14].
- Redraw Fibs as new swings form; "don't force it" [9:09].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- R:R examples: 1:3 (first trade), 1:26 (sniper version) vs 1:4 (higher-TF entry) [8:09, 11:12–12:13].
- No risk % or lot size.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Never trade Fib alone: "Fibonacci is not to be traded by itself" [3:08].
- Don't blindly enter at a level: "I'm not a trader who just tells you it'll reverse off 38.2 or 50 or 61.8" [5:09].
- Wait for the retrace: "price always has to retrace no matter what" [15:15].
- Don't FOMO into impulses: "don't have FOMO and try to get in the trade" [15:15].
- Exit at the extension: "the minute it does that… get out the trade" [8:09].
- Don't force Fibs: "the Fibonacci retracement should be constantly added if you see it fit, don't force it" [9:09].
- Let trades play out: "once you're in a trade relax… have your stop someplace, have it in a proper place and let it play out" [13:14].
