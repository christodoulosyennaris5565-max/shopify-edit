## 1. What this video covers
This is a slide-based risk management and psychology guide. He uses a lab member's story as a case study: the member was up $14K on gold, took no partials, closed with $2.25K, then nearly blew the account. The concrete rules: trade only 1:3 or better (1:2 "if you have to"), with 1:4 as the sweet spot; win rate doesn't matter if R:R is high; increase lot size slowly; risk 2–4% normally (he sometimes takes 5–9% on "amazing" trades); never jump lot size on a streak; take profit or partials; keep a main income. It reflects his 2023 swing approach: stops of 40–60 pips aiming for 180–230 pips. Two gold chart examples and one USDJPY loss are briefly shown.

## 6. Entry rules
- Only take trades whose R:R is at least 1:2, preferably 1:3 or more (see §10).
- He sometimes takes 1:2 "because I got in too late" when highly confident [8:04].
- Don't take random trades that "look good" outside your trading plan [12:07–13:07].
- Entry triggers: not covered in this video.

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Always set a stop and a take profit: "this is why you set a take profit and a stop loss because the market can do one or the other" [21:10].
- Typical stop size is 40–60 pips [20:10].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Targets are about 180–230 pips, giving 1:3 to 1:4+ against the stop [20:10].
- Psychological levels serve as TP: gold "TP would have been 1900… it even got lower than 1900 which is my psychological level my key area" [12:07].
- **Take the money.** In the case study he says "you could have taken partials, you could have gotten out at five thousand…" [0:02]. When to close: "the market will tell you… structure is kind of reversing… we need a little bit of a rally" [16:07].

## 9. Trade management
- **Take partial profits** when well in profit; never let a big winner turn into a loss. "There is no excuse to go from being up 14,000 to almost blowing your account in the same trade" [0:02].
- **Close on signs of reversal:** structure reversing, or the market needing "relief… a rally" after a drop [16:07].
- **"Next play" mentality:** move on immediately after a loss or a missed trade [21:10–22:10].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- **R:R minimum:** "always trade with the one to three plus risk to reward ratio, one to two if you have to" [27:10]. On the slide, 1:1 is red, 1:2 half red/half green, and 1:3 and 1:4 are green. "Winning three and one to four is your good spot" [8:04].
- **Worked math on a $10K account risking $400 per trade (4%):**
  - 1:1, 5 wins and 5 losses = breakeven [7:03].
  - 1:4, 5 wins and 5 losses: −$2,000 + $8,000 = +$6,000 [9:04].
  - 1:4, 3 wins and 7 losses: −$2,800 + $4,800 = +$2,000. "You can have a 30% win rate and still be profitable" [10:05].
- **Win rate:** "win rates don't move me". A 30% win-rate trader taking 1:4 beats a 70% win-rate trader taking 1:1 [10:05–11:06].
- **Lot size progression:** use a "moderate lot size where you just slowly increase it with time", e.g. 0.5, 0.5, 0.5, and after about four wins that outweigh the losses go to 0.6, then 0.7. With high R:R the account compounds [12:07].
- **Never jump size on a winning streak.** Lab members went from 0.10 to 0.60 lots on trade 7 after six wins in a row and blew their accounts. Example: a 60-pip stop × 0.60 lots = $360 loss [19:09–20:10].
- **Risk % per trade:** "two to four percent is fine, five to eight percent is aggressive". He doesn't risk only 1%. "Sometimes I'll risk three or four. If the trade is amazing I'll even risk five to eight percent, even nine percent, and that's not the norm" [28:11]. On a $100K account he may lose $6–8K per trade but wins $30–40K, so "I can lose two or three and then have two winners and I'm golden" [28:11].
- **Contradictory advice:** keep trading risk "towards the lower mid… most of the lower end of the spectrum" [29:13]. Medium risk is fine in life, not in trading.
- **Main income:** keep a main income while learning. Don't quit your job to trade full-time unless you have saved about two years of bills [25:10–26:10].
- **Recent record:** "my last eight trades… I won seven of them" [18:07].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Trade 1:3+ R:R. "Always trade with the one to three plus risk to reward ratio, one to two if you have to" [27:10].
- Avoid 1:1. "You don't want a one-to-one risk reward ratio because that means you have to be right almost every time" [7:03].
- Win rate is not the goal. "You don't have to win more trades than you lose, you just got to make sure your wins outweigh your losses" [11:06].
- Don't force a trade every day. "You guys think you got to trade every single day" [12:07].
- Scale up lot size slowly. "A moderate lot size where you just slowly increase it with time" [12:07].
- Don't up-size on a streak. "Who told you to add a higher lot size and not 0.01 or 0.04" [19:09].
- Stick to your trading plan. "They take any trade that looks good and don't stay consistent with their trading plan" [12:07].
- Risk range: "two to four percent is fine, five to eight percent is aggressive" [28:11].
- Take profit. "You could have taken partials, you could have gotten out at five thousand" [0:02].
- Move on. "Athletes move on after every single play" [6:03].
- Keep a job. "You need a main income" [25:10].
- Leave personal finances out of trading decisions. "The market doesn't give a f[…] you're in debt" [2:02].
