## 1. What this video covers
Using a GBPAUD daily chart (for clarity; he says he actually analyzes and enters intraday), he teaches three linked ideas: (1) "black chart" trading — all candles one color so you focus on structure and aren't scared out by retracement candles; (2) consolidation breakouts — clone the consolidation range and place the copy below/above the "break stop point" to project the target; and (3) the trendline "A-B-C" (1-2-3) take-profit method — when a trendline with points A, B, C breaks, price heads to C, then B, then A, which become TP1/TP2/TP3. He also covers conservative vs aggressive entries on a trendline/consolidation break, stops placed by structure (not fixed 20-30 pips), re-entries on every lower high/higher low, and Fibonacci 61.8 bounce to the 27 extension. Era: 2020 intraday/swing.

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- **Black charts ("zero dark 30")**: all candles black so up and down candles look the same; learned from his mentor "Sean [Shalee?]", backtested ~1.5–2 years [2:07], [3:08], [4:08].
- **Moving averages**: two MAs, "blue over the red" = bullish; "blue crossed over to red" signals a turn [5:08], [11:13]. Periods not stated. He says moving averages are the only indicator; no MACD, stochastics or RSI [13:15].
- **Trendlines** with points A, B, C; must touch A and B exactly [17:22]-[18:22].
- **Rectangle clone tool** (TradingView) to copy consolidation range [7:09].
- **Fibonacci**: A-B-C drawn from low (A) to high (B); entries off the 61.8 retracement; targets at the 27 extension (−0.27) [14:15], [16:21].
- Bar replay used for demonstration [14:15].
- Support/resistance key levels (previous stopping points) for targets; chart otherwise "naked" [8:09].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Set chart to black candles to remove emotional reaction to opposite-color candles [3:08].
2. Identify structure: consolidation ranges, trendlines (A-B-C points), higher lows/lower highs, MA alignment (blue vs red) [5:08].
3. Don't trade inside consolidation; wait for a break (and optionally a retest) of the consolidation/trendline [5:08], [10:12].
4. On a break, mark the "break stop point": where the breakout leg stopped before the pullback/retest [6:09]-[7:09], [23:25].
5. Clone the consolidation box and place it just below (downtrend) or above (uptrend) the break stop point — not directly at the box edge — to project the target [7:09], [11:13].
6. For trendline breaks, set targets at the previous trendline's points C, then B, then A [12:14], [24:27].
7. Check confluence with prior stopping points (support/resistance) and Fibonacci extension [8:09], [14:15].
8. Drop to lower timeframes (15m) for the actual entry (not detailed here).

## 6. Entry rules
- **Conservative**: wait for a break, a retest, then a "breakthrough"; mark a line where the break stopped, and sell when price closes below / comes back below that line [6:09].
- **Aggressive**: enter the moment price breaks the consolidation/trendline, stop above the last lower high [6:09]-[7:09], [17:22]. He says he's sometimes aggressive, sometimes conservative, and that conservative entries can miss a lot ("about 500 pips" on one example) [18:22]-[19:23].
- Aggressive double-top entry: after a trendline retest forms a double top / lower high, sell, stop just above the top [18:22].
- Re-entries: "Every time I see like a downtrend, a lower high, I'm entering again. Every time I see a higher low in an uptrend, I'm entering again" [20:24].
- Fibonacci entry: bounce off the 61.8 retracement [16:21].
- Order type: not stated (market implied).

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Stop placed by structure, above the last lower high (shorts) / below the last higher low, a few pips beyond [6:09]-[7:09], [17:22].
- Against fixed small stops: "People may tell you, oh, have a 20 or 30 pip stop loss. That's not how it should be... It needs to be cohesive with market structure" [17:22].
- In a consolidation break, the stop would be "above this whole consolidation range" [4:08].
- Small accounts that over-lever (1.00 lot on $300) are forced into 10-pip stops; that's what he avoids [22:25].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- **Consolidation clone**: target = cloned range placed beyond the break stop point; it often lines up with prior stopping points [7:09]-[8:09], [11:13].
- **Previous stopping points**: "I place my take profits previously where price stopped at" [8:09].
- **Trendline A-B-C**: "When price breaks point C, it is going to point B. Period" [12:14]; TP1 at C, TP2 at B, TP3 at A (start of the trendline), TP4 at further level if price breaks through [19:23]-[20:24], [24:27].
- **Fib**: 27 extension target; in the GA example the next TP is ~1.86510, "150 pips above" [14:15].
- He takes his target and gets out even if price keeps going; may re-analyze to get back in [8:09].

## 9. Trade management
- Hit target -> exit fully: "I know my take profit. I'm getting out" [8:09]; not greedy for extra 200 pips [9:11].
- Black charts help avoid closing early on retracement candles; let price hit the stop or TP [3:08]-[4:08].
- If price reaches C and B but not A: "play it by ear, manually get out" [24:27].
- A losing re-entry after four winners is acceptable ("four winning trades and one losing trade potentially is great") [20:24].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- R:R examples: 1:2 on a daily-chart example (acceptable, "it's not one to one. Good"); 1:4 on a re-entry; preference 1:3 [19:23]-[20:24].
- Warns against using 1.00 standard lot on a $300 account [22:25].
- Risk %: Not covered in this video.

## 11. Trades walked through in this video

### Trade 1: GBPAUD short — consolidation break, daily (5:08–9:11)
- Consolidation with MAs up (blue over red), then an evening star [shown on chart]. Break below, retest, then down.
- Conservative: wait for break-retest-break; aggressive: sell on the break, stop above the last lower high.
- Target: cloned consolidation range placed below the break stop point; lines up with past stopping points. Range quoted as about 350 pips ("185 and 181.4"), i.e. roughly 1.8500 to 1.8140 [9:11].
- Result: target hit; he exits there rather than chasing extra 200 pips. Price then formed an inverted head & shoulders and rose back to a prior major level [9:11]-[10:12].

### Trade 2: GBPAUD — second consolidation break and reversal (10:12–12:14)
- Lower highs, consolidation; he clones the range below the candle where the retest stopped (break stop point). Price "melts" into the range and stops at an exact pip match of the earlier range.
- Then higher low, another higher low, and blue MA crosses over red -> downtrend done.

### Trade 3: GBPAUD long — trendline A-B-C break (12:14–14:15)
- Downtrend line with points A, B, C; price breaks above at C (higher low forms).
- Entry on lower timeframe [not detailed]. TP1 = point C area; price retests the MA and continues; TP2 = point B (wick-to-body area); after a double top and a trendline retest, TP3 = point A.
- Fib alternative: A–B Fib; TP1 ~1.85 (point A of trendline) [?], next TP 1.86510, ~150 pips higher; price reached the 27 extension then collapsed [14:15]-[15:20].

### Trade 4: GBPAUD long — trendline + Fib 61.8 (16:21–17:22)
- New trendline; once price bounces, get in; Fib A to B, 61.8 bounce, runs to the 27 extension and comes back down. Entries deferred to sniper-entry videos.

### Trade 5: GBPAUD short — trendline break, aggressive vs conservative (17:22–21:25)
- Trendline breaks down; aggressive sells with stop a few pips above the last high ("a little wide stop loss").
- Retest of trendline forms a double top and lower high; aggressive sell there, stop just above the top. Conservative would wait for a break lower and miss "about 500 pips".
- TP1 = lowest point of the move (prior low) ≈ 1:2 R:R on the daily; TP2/TP3/TP4 = points of the previous trendline (C, B, A).
- Re-entries on each lower high: one with ~1:4 R:R; a fifth re-entry could have been a slight loss — "four winning trades and one losing trade".

### Trade 6: GBPAUD short — final trendline run-through (21:25–22:25)
- Trendline A-B connected; break; TP1, retest, TP2, TP3 achieved; TP4 not at first, consolidation, then TP4 achieved. TPs line up with previous trendline levels ("the market is art").

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Black charts to stop fearing retracements: "You're not scared of retracement candles" [3:08].
- Wait for consolidation to break: "Obviously you want to wait for a break and a retest of a trend line and consolidation" [5:08].
- Clone below the break stop point: "I'd place my consolidation range right below it because the real move hasn't happened yet" [7:09].
- Take profits at prior stopping points: "I place my take profits previously where price stopped at" [8:09].
- Trendline targets: "When price breaks point C, it is going to point B. Period. End of story." [12:14]
- Draw trendlines correctly: "Make sure it touches A, B, and then just draw it straight from there" [18:22].
- Stops by structure, not fixed: "It needs to be cohesive with market structure" [17:22].
- Re-enter on structure: "Every time I see a downtrend, a lower high, I'm entering again" [20:24].
- Don't be greedy: "The market gave me 350 pips. I'm not going to be greedy" [9:11].
- No 1.00 lot on $300 [22:25].
