## 1. What this video covers
His first full strategy walkthrough since early 2023, recorded the day after he spoke at an FX Summit and launched his prop firm. He builds a EURUSD chart from blank: "quarter level" key levels on the weekly, shorter-term 4H levels, a Fibonacci ABC retracement, and explains his entry method (small market order between the 38.2 and 50 retracement, larger pending limit order at the 61.8). He lists the five parts of the strategy (key levels, Fibonacci, trend lines, Japanese candlesticks, moving averages / 8 EMA) and applies the same thinking to GBPAUD, GBPUSD, USDJPY and gold. It is the 2024 swing-trading version; he states that the chart work has not changed since 2023, and only his psychology and execution have changed. Despite the "day trading" title, he describes himself as a swing trader and only describes his old day-trading layout in passing.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- **Style:** swing trader. "I am also a swing Trader" [6:08]. He defines swing trading loosely: "swing trading doesn't necessarily mean holding for two or three weeks it just means longer than a day" [14:18].
- **Expected duration:** a EURUSD retracement move "may take you know one or two weeks" [12:13]. A GBPUSD idea is held "for a good 250 300 Pips" [35:28].
- **Swing timeframes:** weekly to get key levels and trend ("I look at the weekly just to just to get my you know key levels on there see if there's any Trends" [7:08]); daily (he switches to the daily briefly at [8:08]); 4H for shorter-term support/resistance ("these can be looked at as 4our support levels" [11:12]).
- **His old day-trading layout (for contrast):** he "wasn't analyzing on the weekly or the daily as an overall day trader"; he had the 15-minute and 2-hour charts side by side, sometimes the 4-hour [6:08-7:08]. He says the same method "still works as a day trading strategy" [6:08].
- He says he still does "a little bit of intraday trading" [7:08].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Start from a clean chart on TradingView ("strip my charts clean and go from top down analysis" [2:04]).
2. **Weekly:** mark quarter-level key levels ("I look at the market in levels right and levels for me comes in you know quarters" [4:06]): for EURUSD 1.1000, 1.1025, 1.1050, 1.1075 and the major ones he uses here: 1.1000, 1.0750, 1.0500 [4:06-5:07]. Turn each line into a rectangle (zone). Check history to confirm the zone has been respected ("wick wick wick" [5:07]). Label weekly resistance/support.
3. **Identify the trend or consolidation** on the weekly/daily. EURUSD is "one big consolidation area" between 1.10 and 1.05 [7:08-8:08].
4. Note chart patterns (e.g. an inverted head and shoulders inside the range [10:12]) and dollar context (DXY) and news/fundamentals.
5. **Drop to the 4H** and mark shorter-term support/resistance boxes where price wicked several times [11:12-12:13].
6. **Draw a Fibonacci ABC** on the current swing to find the retracement zone (38.2-61.8) and the extension target (-0.27 / -0.618) [11:12-13:14].
7. **Look for trend lines** (only if there is a real trend; "you can try to force a trend line right here but it's too tight" [16:22]).
8. **Check candlesticks and the 8 EMA** for confirmation. "I use as many confluences as possible when it comes to making an actual bias" [17:22].
9. **Build if-then scenarios** rather than a prediction: "if the market does this then I'll do that" [36:28].
10. Set alerts at the area and let the market come to you ("I don't chase the market I let the market come to me" [19:24]).

## 6. Entry rules
- **Location:** a Fibonacci retracement of the latest leg, in the direction of his bias, ideally at or near a key level / trend line.
- **Market execution:** "I usually will Market order somewhere between the 38.2 and the 50 and just be in a little bit of draw down because I'm a swing Trader" [13:14]. Done with a **low lot size**.
- **Pending limit order:** at the 61.8 retracement, "nine times out of 10 price usually will retrace at the highest point at 61.8" [15:20]. Done with a **high lot size** because there is less drawdown at that price.
- Rule as he dictates it: "on Market executions I enter with a low lot size ... on sell limits ... pending orders I am enter with a high lot size" [13:14-14:18].
- The 78.6 is treated as the point where the idea is failing: "if it get to the 78.6 and at that point we may as well get out the trade it price usually doesn't retrace that high" [13:14].
- So there are typically **two entries** on one idea: a small market order at 38.2-50 plus a larger limit at 61.8.
- **GBPAUD example:** a pending **buy limit** at a mid key level that is also a trend-line touch (ABCD) [18:24].
- **GBPUSD example:** wait for a break below and a **retest of the trend line**, then enter, with a Japanese candlestick reversal (e.g. a shooting star) as the trigger [35:28-36:28].
- **News filter:** "if I haven't gotten in the trade yet and I see a trade looks you know beautiful but news is coming out in 20 minutes I won't get in the trade" [29:28].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Stop goes above/below the Fibonacci structure (for the EURUSD short, above the 61.8/78.6 area; "my stop loss is probably somewhere up here" [13:14], exact price shown on chart, not stated).
- With the limit entry at 61.8 the stop is "much much tighter" because the entry is closer to the stop [13:14].
- No pip size is given in this video.

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Targets are Fibonacci extensions: "TP is right here this 27 or the 61 or right in the middle" [13:14] (i.e. between the -0.27 and -0.618 extensions).
- Next key level / opposite end of the range (e.g. GBPAUD back to the top of the consolidation [18:24]; EURUSD 1.0750, 1.05).
- **R:R quoted:** market execution between 38.2 and 50 gives "around one in 2.8 one and3 risk reward ratio"; the 61.8 sell limit gives "a 1 and eight risk reward ratio" [13:14].
- GBPUSD: hold "250 300 Pips" to trend-line point B, then point A [35:28].

## 9. Trade management
- **Accepting drawdown:** the market order is expected to go into drawdown before working: "how many times you guys as a Trader got in a trade and the market was in draw down for 8 hours straight and ... you just got out with a break even and then the market went exactly in your direction" [14:18]. He blames impatience, not "smart money stop hunting".
- **Partials:** in the GBPUSD scenario, if price breaks the first target level "I'm going to take partial profits and hold to here" [36:28] (percentage not stated).
- **Early full exit on reversal pattern:** "if it just reverses and gives me like a ... W or ... inverted Head and Shoulders a Morning Star anything like that then okay yeah I'm stopping right here taking all profits" [36:28].
- **News while in a trade:** "if I'm already in a trade and news is coming out like a major news announcement whether it's CPI NFP fomc anything like that I won't get out the trade unless it's completely drastic" [28:27].
- **Alerts:** "alerts are my best friends where if I feel like I'm tired or anything I will set an alert and it will text my phone when the market is coming to my area" [29:28-30:28].
- Fundamentals check: read the TradingView/ForexLive news (technical, fundamental, sentiment analysis); "it takes you five minutes" [29:28].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- Lot size is varied by entry type (small on market orders, large on pending limits) [13:14].
- Grow by increasing lot size over time on a working style: "you'll see your your profits you know scale up if you just increase the lot size as time goes on" [4:06].
- **Profit expectations:** $250,000 a year puts you in the top 1%; $10,000 a month is "great" [20:24-22:25]. Prop firm math: "10% of this right and that's $25,000 of a $250,000 account ... do that four times there's your 100K" [25:26-26:26].
- **Capital:** "if you have no money you need to get ... funded"; "10% on a ... $25,000 account $100,000 account is a lot more money than 100% on a $100 account" [37:28].
- Do not trade with rent money; have another income (he babysat) [23:25-24:25].
- No risk % per trade stated in this video.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Stick to the one style that works: "completely eradicate that other style and just focus on the one that's working" [3:04-4:06].
- Treat levels as zones: "I use this as an area" [4:06].
- Small size on market orders, big size on limits: "on Market executions I enter with a low lot size ... pending orders I am enter with a high lot size" [13:14-14:18].
- Accept drawdown on swing entries: "you guys were not patient enough this is why I adapted to the swing trading mindset" [14:18].
- Use multiple confluences: "I use as many confluences as possible when it comes to making an actual bias" [17:22].
- Don't force trend lines: "you can try to force a trend line right here but it's too tight" [16:22].
- Don't chase: "I don't chase the market I let the market come to me" [19:24].
- No entries right before news: "news is coming out in 20 minutes I won't get in the trade" [29:28].
- Don't exit on news unless drastic: "I won't get out the trade unless it's completely drastic" [28:27].
- Think in if-then terms: "talk in terms of if the market does this then I'll do that" [37:28].
- Get funded if undercapitalized: "if you have no money you need to get ... funded" [37:28].
