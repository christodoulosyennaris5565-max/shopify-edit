## 1. What this video covers
A Dubai vlog in which he places and manages a live gold (XAUUSD) long. He identifies a daily-timeframe Fibonacci ABC pullback to the 61.8, waits for a 4H candle close as confirmation, and enters with a small market order first. He then layers in larger pending orders: a buy limit below price and a buy stop above a counter-trend line / inverse head and shoulders neckline. He states lot sizes, potential profit and loss in dollars, and a ~1:5 R:R on the starter position. Later he reports the floating P/L ($20k, $27k, $30k, $40k peak, then back to $10k), his plan to move the stop into profit after a higher low prints, and letting the New York session play out. The video ends without a final result. Promotional segments (a $47 3-day boot camp) are not documented.

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. **Daily structure:** "I can see price higher low in comparison to this low higher low did another higher low right here" [0:01]; the daily is in an uptrend making higher lows.
2. **Daily Fibonacci ABC:** "I see a Fibonacci clear as day a BC is right to 61.8" [0:01-1:02]; the C leg has pulled back to the 61.8.
3. **Drop to the 4H** for the confirmation candle: he wants the current 4H candle, which looked like a doji, to close as a doji / small body [1:02].
4. **Check 1H and lower** for structure: the 1H "looks kind of ugly", so he expects some consolidation "before breaking through this counter trend line and then skyrocketing up to our projection of 2681" [1:02].
5. **Identify the trigger levels:** a break above 2633 (counter-trend line / inverse H&S neckline area) for a buy stop; a lower level (2623) for a buy limit "in case it wants to get that low" [2:02-4:10].
6. **Plan the layered entries** (see section 6) to "trap price" from both sides.

## 6. Entry rules
- **Confirmation needed:** "if this doji can close and print in the next 10 minutes ... that's a confirmation I need to actually get in" [1:02]. Candle-body rule: "waiting to see how this 4-Hour Candlestick wants to close um whether it wants to close 50% of the body or 10 20% cuz if we get close to 10 or 20% Then um we're entering fully fully" [2:02]. (A small body, 10-20%, i.e. a doji-like close = full entry.)
- **Starter position before confirmation, with small size:** "there's a chance I may get in now with a lower lot size like I usually do" [1:02]; "price wants to pull up then I'm in the trade regardless um and then scale in once I get that extra extra extra confirmation" [2:02].
- **Three order types used together ("trap price"):** "I'm going to get a buy limit a market execution and then a buy stop to trap price cuz I really believe it's about to pull up" [5:11].
  1. **Market execution:** 2.5 lots [5:11].
  2. **Buy limit:** 5 lots at 2623 (later replaced; see below) [3:09-4:10].
  3. **Buy stop:** 10 lots at 2634, just above 2633, "if I can break above 2633 that'd be great" [2:02, 6:11].
- **Adjustment:** he later moved the buy limit up to "maybe 10 Pips below price" with 10 lots, to get "the last little bit of draw down I possibly can get" [9:12-10:12]; if price hesitated and didn't clear those pips he would market-execute instead [9:12]. That limit filled shortly after [10:12].

## 9. Trade management
- **Scale in:** small first, add after confirmation and with pending orders at better/confirmed prices [2:02, 5:11].
- **Minimize drawdown:** "I like to minimize draw down as much as possible" [10:12]; that is why he used a limit a little below price rather than a second market order.
- **Move stop to profit after a higher low:** "I'm waiting for a higher low to be printed and then once that higher low is printed then I can move my stop loss into profit so now it's a complete risk- free trade" [14:22].
- **Hold through red candles:** "I'm up $30,000 and I've seen a bunch of red candles I could have gotten out at any point in time ... but it's like I feel like it's going to go higher" [14:22].
- **Let the New York session play out** to 5 pm New York before judging [15:23].
- **Close and re-enter option:** after it fell back to $10k: "I may close the trade and then reenter or watch it go back up" [15:23]; "I can close at 10 and get back in because gold is looking crazy right now" [16:23].
- He comments on greed at the start: "I'm up a certain amount of money if I would just close right now I could go on vacation ... but I don't want to close cuz I want to double that and then you go to sleep you wake up and now you just lost it all ... you can't just be greedy" [0:01].
