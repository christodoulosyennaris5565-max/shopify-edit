## 1. What this video covers
A short price-action / market-structure lesson on one AUDUSD 4-hour/2-hour example. He shows how he ignores the higher-timeframe (monthly/weekly) trend and trades the most recent structure, waits for a counter-trend pullback to reach a key level and consolidate, and then enters on a stack of confluences (higher low, moving-average crossover, break of the counter-trend line, Marubozu momentum candle). Stop goes below the last higher low and the target is the next key level, giving him a claimed 1:18 R:R. He also explains "mid-levels" (halfway between two key levels) and why he draws zones rather than lines. This is the 2020 level-to-level intraday/swing version of his strategy.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- Style for this strategy: "not a swing trade" but "in the middle of intraday and swing trading" — holding "maybe a couple of hours to one or two days" because price may not reach the next level within an hour or two [10:17–11:18].
- He says he also has a separate scalping strategy and an intraday strategy (not shown) [10:17].
- Analysis on the 4-hour chart, drop to a 2-hour to watch the pullback [3:07]. Explicitly does **not** start from monthly/weekly for trend in this example: "We're looking at the most recent price action" [3:07].
- He says he is not a fixed-hours trader: "I'm not like trading randomly… okay I only trade from 6 to 8" — i.e. he waits for price to reach a level rather than trading a time window [4:08].

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- Moving averages are the only indicator: "I don't use indicators except moving averages; that is the only indicator I use" [3:07]. Periods not stated here (two MAs that cross; colours not stated).
- Horizontal support/resistance key levels, drawn as **zones** (boxes around the line), plus **mid-levels** halfway between two key levels (e.g. 0.6250 between 0.6000 and 0.6500) [8:14–9:16].
- Fibonacci can be used just to measure the 50% midpoint, but he prefers to draw a horizontal mid-level line [9:16].
- Counter-trend trend line (drawn across the pullback) [7:14].
- Consolidation "box" drawn around a ranging area [5:09].
- Candlesticks: Marubozu ("Mero BOSU"/"Amir Bolton" in captions) = momentum candle [7:14].
- Rejects RSI, volume, Bollinger Bands, harmonic scanners, software: "all that stuff is fake" [11:18].
- Pays attention to fundamentals (no specifics) [11:18].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Open the 4-hour chart and read the **most recent** trend from structure and the direction of the MAs (here: uptrend, MAs pointing up) [3:07].
2. Note the last breakout of resistance and that price has not yet retested it [3:07].
3. When price pulls back (counter-trend: lower highs/lower lows), do not trade the counter-trend; expect it to stop and resume the main trend [4:08].
4. Drop to the 2-hour and watch price as it reaches the key level/zone [3:07–4:08].
5. At a major level price will either reverse or break through — wait to see which [4:08].
6. If price consolidates at the level with many wicks (he says 8–15 wicks), expect a reversal, not a break: "price will not get to a level, consolidate, have 8 to 15 wicks and then keep going in that direction" [4:08–5:09]. Draw a consolidation box.
7. Count rejections of the zone (here 8 rejections on 2-hour candles = 16 hours) as evidence the zone holds [5:09–6:11].
8. Look for the higher low (confirming the uptrend resumes) [6:11].
9. Stack confluences: higher low → MA crossover → break of the counter-trend line → break made with a Marubozu momentum candle [7:14].
10. Enter, stop below last higher low, target the next key level (with the mid-level as an intermediate reaction point) [8:14–9:16].

## 6. Entry rules
- Only trade in the direction of the most recent trend; enter after the counter-trend pullback ends at a level [3:07–4:08].
- Confluences listed (in order): (1) structure higher low, (2) MA crossover, (3) break of the counter-trend line, (4) break done with a Marubozu momentum candle [7:14].
- Aggressive entry: as soon as you see the MA crossover plus the higher low [7:14–8:14].
- Patient entry: wait for the Marubozu candle to close above the counter-trend line [8:14]. He calls himself "more aggressive" and enters on the Marubozu close + MA crossover [8:14].
- Market order on candle close (implied; order type not explicitly named).
- Additional entries: "you could have got a second entry right here or right here" on later retracements (e.g. at the mid-level) — details reserved for his paid course [9:16–10:17].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- "Stop-loss below the last higher low" [8:14].
- General: when trading levels "your stop-loss always goes below that level or above that level" (below for an uptrend, above for a downtrend) [10:17].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- "My take profit level is the next level" [8:14]. Here this gave 1:18 R:R.
- Mid-level (halfway between key levels) is where price may retrace on the way — here 0.6250 [8:14–9:16].
- Targets are the mid-level or the very next level, so R:R is "always higher than a one to three" [10:17].

## 9. Trade management
- Expect a retracement at the mid-level on the way to target; it does not invalidate the trade [8:14–9:16].
- Can add a second entry on the retest of the mid-level [9:16–10:17].
- No break-even/partials discussed in this video.

## 10. Risk management and position sizing

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Don't trade counter-trends: "this is the kind of thing we do not trade, counter trends" [4:08].
- Use only recent price action for trend, not monthly/weekly: "No, I'm not doing that, we're looking at the most recent price action" [3:07].
- At a major level price does one of two things: "it is gonna reverse or it's gonna break through it. That is what I trade and what I wait on" [4:08].
- Consolidation with many wicks at a level means reversal: "if price starts consolidating at a level, chances are it's gonna reverse" [4:08].
- Marubozu = continuation: "if you see a Marubozu, chances are it's gonna continue in that same direction" [7:14].
- Draw zones, not lines: "price does not stop at an exact number every single time, it'll stop in the area around it" [9:16].
- Have mid-levels: "my mid levels that I preach that everybody have in the middle of their support and resistance" [8:14].
- Prioritise S/R above all tools: "I place significant importance on support and resistance" [10:17].
- No R:R below 1:2: "I'm not trading one to one, one to two" [8:14].
- No indicators other than MAs: "no like RSI, volume… Bollinger Bands, I don't use any of them" [11:18].
