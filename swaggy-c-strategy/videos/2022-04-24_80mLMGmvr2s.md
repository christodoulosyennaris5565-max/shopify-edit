## 1. What this video covers
A vlog episode; the only trading content is a ~10-minute market segment recorded during the Russia–Ukraine war (gold at ~$2,000, so likely filmed in early March 2022 even though uploaded in April). He goes through gold, GBPAUD, GBPJPY, GBPUSD, EURUSD and AUDUSD, explaining that in a crisis the market trends hard in one direction (400–600 pips a day) and is driven "99.9%" by fundamentals and sentiment, so he reads FXStreet and DailyFX news before trading. Technically, he points to a daily **50 / 61.8 Fibonacci retracement**, a **counter-trendline break**, and level-to-level moves with retests; when there is no retracement, he drops to the **15-minute / 5-minute** to find an entry. Elsewhere he says he needs only "about 30 minutes a day or less" to analyze, place trades and let them run. The rest (academy launch, trading journal, his brother, Puerto Rico, industry collabs) is not strategy.

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Assess the macro/news backdrop first. In crisis periods (Brexit, the pandemic, Russia–Ukraine) the market trends in one direction for weeks; technical levels still matter but "don't expect it to just hold up like it would during a regular week" [15:14].
2. Read FXStreet/DailyFX articles and what leaders/analysts say (sentiment analysis) and form a directional bias [15:14–17:15].
3. Check the weekly chart for the big picture level-to-level path (e.g. gold 1870 → 1915 → 2000) [12:12].
4. On the daily, identify the pullback: a 50 or 61.8 Fib retracement, then a break of the counter-trendline, then a move to the next level, retest of the broken level, next level, etc. [13:14].
5. Compare with similar past cycles (March 2020 pandemic: "the pairs that went down are going down again; the pairs that shot up are going up again") [17:15].
6. If there is a real retracement, trade the counter-trendline break in the trend direction (AUDUSD example) [20:17].
7. If there is no real retracement, drop to the 15m/5m and look for "the smallest chance of retracement" to enter [20:17].

## 6. Entry rules
- With-trend entry after a pullback: daily 50/61.8 retracement, then a **counter-trendline break** in the trend direction [13:14], [20:17].
- No-pullback markets: "You got to go to a 15 minute, five minute time frame and just make sure you are seeing like where there's the smallest chance of retracement" [20:17].
- Fundamental filter: trade in the direction the news/sentiment supports (buy gold, buy AUD, sell GBP and EUR in this crisis).
- Order type: not covered.
