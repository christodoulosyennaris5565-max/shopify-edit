## 1. What this video covers
Sniper entries part 2. He restates that his strategy is "purely price action and market structure": he trades only from support/resistance (supply/demand) key levels. Entries come at a rejection of a key level or at a break-and-retest; he then scales in on each new lower high (downtrend) or higher low (uptrend), moving all stops to make earlier positions risk-free. Examples on EURUSD, GBPUSD and GBPAUD, plus Fibonacci ABCD/-27 extension as an alternative target and the rule to exit manually if structure breaks before target. This is the 2020 intraday/swing version.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes

- Example charts: 1-hour (GBPUSD), 4-hour pattern then 15- or 5-minute entry (GBPAUD) [9:14, 12:14].
- "I'm immediately looking to scale in on a 15 or 5 minute time frame" after a 4-hour pattern [12:14].

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- Support/resistance key levels ("supply and demand zones"), "the ones the banks use" [1:01–2:01].
- Moving averages (crossover is a confluence; "really tight" MAs = consolidation). Colours mentioned: "blue crossed over to red" [5:07]. Periods not stated.
- Fibonacci retracement with levels incl. 61.8 and the **-27 extension** (ABCD): used as an alternative/confirming take-profit [6:11–7:12, 14:15].
- Candlestick patterns: doji, Marubozu (momentum candle), shooting star, bearish engulfing ("big red over a small green/blue"), evening star, hammer [3:05, 4:06, 8:14, 12:14, 13:15].
- Consolidation boxes [5:07].
- Price alerts on levels (e.g. orange alert line at 1.95 on GBPAUD) [13:15–14:15].
- "I don't have a lot of stuff on my charts" [7:12].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. On a blank chart, the first thing: find the key levels (S/R) [1:01–2:01].
2. Only look to trade when price reaches a key level, not in between [2:01].
3. At resistance/support price either breaks through or rejects — read which: many dojis/consolidation = rejection ("it won't place eight dojis in a row and then break the level") [2:01–3:05].
4. Draw a box around the consolidation; watch for tight MAs (consolidation) [5:07].
5. Look for structure change (higher low / lower high), MA crossover, momentum candle → entry [3:05, 5:07].
6. For higher-timeframe patterns (e.g. 4-hour evening star), drop to 15m/5m to time the entry [12:14].
7. Target the next key level (or Fib -27 extension); stop at last structure point beyond the level.
8. Scale in on each new lower high/higher low and trail stops [4:06–5:07].

## 6. Entry rules
- "Your entry should be always at a rejection of a key level or a breakthrough and a retest" [4:06–5:07].
- Rejection entry confluences: consolidation box at the level, MA crossover, momentum candle (Marubozu) [3:05, 5:07].
- Uptrend example confluences numbered: (1) consolidation, (2) higher low / MA crossover ("blue crossed over to red"), then a huge momentum candle = entry [5:07–6:11].
- Bearish engulfing after a lower high forms = short entry [8:14].
- Evening star on 4-hour at a level → 15m/5m: MA crossover off a doji = entry [12:14–13:15].
- Scaling in: "if you're scaling in a downtrend, every time price makes a lower high, scale in, and in an uptrend every time it makes a higher low, scale in" [5:07]. Second entry e.g. at the close of a shooting star; third on next MA crossover + lower high [4:06].
- No bias: if market breaks resistance he buys, if it rejects he sells — "I adapt" [9:14–10:14].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Stop beyond the level/structure: above the rejection area for shorts, "right below" the higher low for longs [3:05, 6:11].
- On GBPAUD evening-star short, stop above the level price was failing to break [12:14].
- Align stops (and targets) with structure, not anywhere: "they place their stop-loss anywhere and take profit anywhere as opposed to aligning it with structure" [7:12].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Next key level ("level to level") [3:05, 6:11].
- Examples: 1:13, 1:4, 1:4, ~1:8 [?], 1:5.5 [3:05, 6:11, 9:14, 13:15, 14:15].
- GBPUSD next level 1.2250 ("122 five flat") [9:14]. GBPAUD target "two flat" = 2.0000 [14:15].
- Alternative: Fibonacci -27 extension of the A-B-C swing (C at 61.8) [6:11–7:12, 14:15]. If price gets within a few pips of the target, close manually [6:11].
- Minimum: "I don't trade any reward that's less than one to three" [9:14].

## 9. Trade management
- When price moves in favour, pulls back and continues, move the stop into profit to make it "risk free": "I'm looking to do two things: I'm gonna move my stop loss into profit… [and] I'm looking to scale in" [3:05–4:06].
- Trail the stop to each new lower high (downtrend) / higher low (uptrend) [6:11, 11:14].
- With multiple scaled positions, all stops move together to the latest structure point; earlier trades become risk-free, the newest one may still be at risk [4:06].
- Exit manually if structure breaks before target (e.g. in a downtrend price fails to make a new lower low and forms a higher low): "we are ditching out of the trade, closing it in profit" [10:14–11:14].
- At target, close and watch whether the level rejects or breaks [13:15].
- Uses alerts at levels [13:15].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- Never below 1:3 R:R [9:14].
- Worst case after moving stops: "I won a few hundred dollars" depending on size [4:06].
- No risk % or lot sizes given.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Trade only levels: "I literally trade off support and resistance… I don't trade anything else" [1:01].
- Entry location: "your entry should be always at a rejection of a key level or a breakthrough and a retest" [4:06–5:07].
- Rejection tell: "it won't place eight dojis in a row and then break the level" [3:05].
- Scale in with structure: "every time price makes a lower high, scale in" [5:07].
- Move stop to risk-free: "a lot of people forget to use that trick" [6:11].
- Minimum R:R: "I don't trade any reward that's less than one to three" [9:14].
- No fixed bias: "I don't have a preconceived notion of the market… I just adapt and react to it" [10:14].
- Exit on structure break: "the minute it does this and breaks structure… we are ditching out of the trade" [11:14].
- Target isn't sacred: "that's just my take profit… no, it does not have to go there" [10:14].
