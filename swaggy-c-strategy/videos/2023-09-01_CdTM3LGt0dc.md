## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Know the pair intimately; trade one or two pairs [1:02].
2. Mark the major psychological levels or key areas [2:02, 7:04].
3. At a level, look for a double bottom or congestion, where price refuses to break [1:02].
4. Wait for the MA crossover in the new direction [2:02].
5. Look for a higher low (for a long), which shows "market sentiment has switched over" [2:02]. Keep tracking higher lows to confirm "structure has not been broken yet" [2:02].
6. **Range variant (GBPAUD):** at a major psychological level, an immediate reversal followed by congestion. "Simply draw a box on it and see what it wants to do" [7:04]. Expect a fake wick beyond the level before the real move. Check whether the MAs cross before the box breaks [8:06].
7. Enter (see §6). Set TP1 and TP2 at psychological levels.
8. Check the fundamental context, e.g. gold as a safe haven if a US recession is confirmed [4:03].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Trade one or two pairs. "I always advocate to trade one or two pairs so you know everything it does" [1:02].
- A higher low after congestion means a shift. "When you see congestion… and then it gives you a higher low understand market sentiment has switched over" [2:02].
- Box the consolidation at major levels. "Simply draw a box on it and see what it wants to do" [7:04].
- Expect fakeouts. "If price is going to break a key level it's going to try to wick above it, come back down… fake people out" [7:04].
- Breaks get retested. "Every time price breaks an area it is going to retest, no matter what" [8:06].
- Take partials. "You can take half profits now, maybe 70% profits now, let the others run" [5:03].
- Let trades play out through drawdown. "Understanding drawdown happens and then again getting back into profit" [11:07].
- Any style works. "All trading styles work, it just depends on who's implementing them" [10:07].
