## 1. What this video covers
This is mostly a personal story: being on Big Brother hurt his trading, and an argument with his wife pushed him to commit fully. The strategy-relevant part is his list of "a-ha" factors from late 2018. He discovered psychological levels in 2017. He studied Sean Lee's public 5K→630K challenge every day for 2–3 months: the lot sizes, entry timing and pairs, which is where his GBPAUD and gold focus comes from. He stopped over-studying technicals and focused on risk management. And he studied for an hour a day. He says his technicals have not changed since he started. No trades are walked through.

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- The breakthrough was risk management, not technicals: "it was a psychology and risk management part of it all" [14:08–15:08].
- The pattern he fixed: making $5K in January and $6K in February, then losing in March and breaking even in April, because of "risking too much, not being okay with making 5K every single month… you make 5K one month, all of a sudden you're trying to double it the next one" [15:08].
- He studied Sean Lee's challenge lot sizes to learn how a small account can be compounded [14:08].
- **Growth claims:** "3K then 10K, and then that 14K challenge, check into 70K and then 80K and then 100K" [8:05]. He also says he had $14,000 after taxes and rent and "took that six thousand dollar account to 300K and 400K and 500K" (late 2018) [17:09–18:09]. The two figures are inconsistent as stated.
- **Rule on scaling life around income:** "once trading matches your bills, keep going. Once trading doubles your bills, then you can look at… reward[ing] myself" with something luxurious or a week or two off [9:05].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Study a proven account's details: "I literally studied that challenge every single almost every single day for two three months straight… the lot sizes he used, when he entered, the pairs he used" [14:08].
- Don't over-study technicals: "I stopped studying technicals way too much… technicals has not changed" [14:08].
- Focus on risk: "stop focusing on technicals too much and focus on the risk management" [15:08].
- Study daily: "I made sure to at least make time one hour a day" [15:08].
- Don't take long breaks from the charts. During "think weeks" off he still opens a chart and keeps up with the news. A full isolation of about 8 months hurt his skill [4:02, 10:05].
- Reward yourself only once trading doubles your bills [9:05].
