## 1. What this video covers
A mostly psychological talk for small-account traders. The concrete rules: treat a small account as a small account — take profit when the market gives 30–70 pips (don't try to ride to 200–300 pips); small accounts suit scalping/intraday, not swing trading; have an exit plan with daily/weekly money goals; and don't over-leverage (0.50 lots with a 30-pip stop on $300 risks half the account). He also explains his Academy order (brokers/live accounts only in the last series). Reflects 2020 era.

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Small account: "you need to take profit if the market gives you 30 to 50 pips" [1:05]; "if you are up 30 to 50 to 70 pips and it's X amount of dollars, okay, take profit" [7:11].
- Not on every small profit: "the best way to build a small account is not to just get in a trade and take profit every single time you're in profit, no" — look at the percentages and pip count [6:10]-[7:11].
- Percent-based: e.g. up $500 on a $250 account = 200% gain; take it [3:07].

## 9. Trade management
- Have an exit plan with goals: "how much money do I want to make by the end of the week, by the end of the day" and stick to it [7:11].
- Treat floating profit like cash on the table [3:07]-[4:07].
- Example of greed: weekly goal $1,000, up $1,200, held for $2,500, closed at $400, then lost it [12:17].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- "You cannot use a 0.50 with a 30 pip stop loss with $300 in your account" — that's $150 risk, half the account [8:14].
- Use small lots such as 0.10, 0.05, 0.15 ("a mini lot") instead of 0.50 or 1.00 [8:14].
- Don't over-leverage to make money faster; repeated blown accounts = should not be on a live account [8:14]-[9:16].
- Build in milestones ($1k, $5k, $10k, $25k, $50k, $100k) and celebrate each [10:17]-[11:17].
- His own history: losing in year one; $2,000–3,500/month in late 2016/early 2017 [6:10].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Take 30-70 pips on small accounts: "You need to take profit if the market gives you 30 to 50 pips" [1:05].
- Small accounts aren't for swing trading: "You don't even have the account size to be a swing trader" [1:05]-[2:05].
- Exit plan: "You got to have an actual plan on, like, an exit plan" [7:11].
- Leverage: "You cannot use a 0.50 with a 30 pip stop loss with 300 in your account" [8:14].
- Don't flip: "Build your account, don't try to flip it" [4:07].
- No revenge trading, no greed, few indicators [10:17].
