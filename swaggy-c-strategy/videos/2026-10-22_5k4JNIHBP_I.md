## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- **Don't yolo.** He defines "yolo" as "putting a crazy high lot size and just guessing" [1:05]. His firm still pays yolo traders but warns them, and "leverage may have to be lower if you're going to just guess and yolo your trades" [1:05].
- **Account size over trading harder:** "Sometimes the answer isn't trading better. Sometimes you're already trading good enough. You're just on too low of an account size" [8:07].
- **Return framing:** 10%/month is the benchmark he uses. 10% on 25K = $2,500/month; on 500K = $50,000; on $1M = $100,000; on $2M = $200,000 [7:07]. He mentions traders on his firm making "10% 20% 25% even 50%" on small accounts.
- **The plan (Q4 2026 into Q1 2027):** "find one or two accounts that fit you. Get both of them. Make sure they're six figure accounts. Even a 100K account is better than nothing. Spend the next 3 to four months trading on these accounts… Don't yolo your trades and try to get a payout day one because you may end up breaching your account. Focus on being a consistent trader" [8:07].
- **Student example ($471K, January 2026):** +66% on a 500K one-step account ($6,300 cost) and +137% on a 100K instant-funding account ($6,300 cost). That is about $12.6K of fees for $471K profit. He scales it: the same % on $1M/$2M accounts would be about $2.69M [5:06–6:06]. The same trader is now at $130K on a new 100K account (+30%). Another trader, "Alfredo", is +12% on 100K (a $12K payout). Another is +6% on 5K ($300) [6:06–7:07].
- **Firm offerings (context):** instant funding now at 100K, 250K, 500K, $1M, $1.5M, $2M; one-step and two-step up to $1M, $1.5M, $2M [4:06]. Previously 100K two-step was about $900 and 500K one-step $6,300 [1:05–2:05]. New prices were shown on screen, not stated. Promo test runs "up until the 25th" [9:08].
- No per-trade risk % or drawdown limits stated.
