## 1. What this video covers
He reads 20 (plus one bonus) psychology lessons from trading books, mostly "Trading in the Zone" (Mark Douglas), "Market Wizards" (the Marcus story), and "The Forex Mindset" (Jared Martinez), and comments on each. The strategy-relevant parts are short. He gives his win rate, says his R:R is always 1:3 and he takes every 1:3 setup, lists what a written trading plan must contain, and says to check the downside before every trade (never risk more than 2/5/10%). He also stresses committing to an exit and stop on every trade, being selective, the if-then mindset, and cutting a trade that violently reverses. This reflects his 2024 swing-trading era.

## 6. Entry rules
- **Take every valid 1:3 setup:** "my risk to word ratio no matter what is 1 to three if I see a 1 to three setup I'm going to trade it" [11:10].
- **Be selective:** wait for trades "in which all the key elements line up in One Direction"; marginal trades are "more to do with entertainment than trading success" (Market Wizards quote, endorsed) [15:11-16:14].
- **Trust your own bias** over your community or mentor [8:09-9:10].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- **Always have an exit / stop:** commit "to an exit point on every trade"; "protective stops like stop losses are very important because they force this commitment on the trader" [13:10-14:11].
- **Cut losers that violently reverse:** don't "breathe the sigh of relief" on small pullbacks; "sometimes you may want to just cut your losses cuz you know it's a loser because ... it violently turned against you" [27:25-28:28]. He accepts the alternative of "just letting it hit your stop loss" [27:25].
- Liquidate positions to regain mental clarity when losing and confused (Market Wizards, endorsed) [14:11].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Fixed **1:3 R:R** [11:10]. No pip targets in this video.

## 9. Trade management
- Cut losers early when the market violently reverses through your entry (see section 7) [27:25-28:28].
- **Withdraw and reward yourself:** "every time you make money I don't care if it's $30 take 12 out and go buy something with it" [33:34].
- No break-even/partial rules in this video.

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- **Win rate:** "I want to say I have like a 80 or maybe even a 70% win" [1:02] (as captioned, uncertain). He uses a webinar attendee's "65%" as the worked example: "you take 10 trades you're going to win six ... or six and a half seven" [2:03]; "I can lose the next five trades but out of 20 that's my 35%" [2:03].
- **R:R:** always 1:3 [11:10].
- **Max risk / check the downside:** before entering, ask "how much can I lose if I use a standard lot if I use a two lot if I use a three lot am I going to blow my account ... am I not risking more than 10% more than 5% more than 2% if I'm not doing that place the trade" [23:20]. "There should be no reason you should be blowing your account" [23:20].
- **Money you can afford to lose:** trade only with money that has "little or no value in your life" [28:28]; he funded his early trading with $4-6k/month from babysitting [29:29].
- **Written trading plan required:** a document with "a detailed list on what you're looking for in terms of setups how many Pips you're looking for the pairs you only trade the time frames you trade on the sessions you trade on what news affects the certain pairs ... and like if I if this happens I do this and like outline your psychology in certain situations" [12:10-13:10]. "Metrics are your best friend" [13:10].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Think in probabilities: "you take 10 trades you're going to win six" [2:03].
- Fixed R:R: "my risk to word ratio no matter what is 1 to three if I see a 1 to three setup I'm going to trade it" [11:10].
- Written rules: "if you don't have any written rules for making a trade then everything about that trade is a mistake" [12:10].
- Track everything: "metrics are your best friend" [13:10].
- Commit to an exit: "committing to an exit point on every trade" [13:10-14:11].
- Be selective: wait for "all the key elements line up in One Direction" [15:11].
- Check the downside: "am I not risking more than 10% more than 5% more than 2%" [23:20].
- Cut violent reversals: "no more sigh reliefs cut your losses" [27:25].
- If-then mindset: "if the market does this then I would do that" [36:39].
- Withdraw profits: "every time you make money ... take 12 out and go buy something with it" [33:34].
