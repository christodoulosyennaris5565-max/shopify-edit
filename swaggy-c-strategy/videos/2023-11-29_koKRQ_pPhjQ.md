## 1. What this video covers
This is three top-down analysis lessons in a row, each starting from a blank chart: GBPAUD (0:00–19:00), AUDUSD (19:28–37:40) and GBPUSD (37:42–end). He explains how he marks monthly, weekly and daily levels with round numbers and "quarters", then adds the levels where price actually reacted, trendlines, chart patterns, Fibonacci retracements and extensions, the 8 and 14 EMAs and Heikin Ashi candles. He then sets a bias and waits for an entry, or sets an alert. He says it shows "how I trade in 2022" and calls it an update of what he taught in his "Gold Academy". The GBPAUD lesson includes a full worked long setup with R:R figures for each target and a step-by-step trailing stop. All three lessons push swing trading over intraday counter-trend trading.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- **Style:** swing trading. He holds a few positions ("1 2 3 4 five trades") and lets them run through pullbacks instead of taking many intraday trades. "This is why I like swing trading… once you get in right here you can just hold it hold it hold it" [15:24].
- **Weekly:** looks at roughly the last 12 weeks (one quarter, about 3 months) only. Older history "does absolutely nothing" for the weekly read [1:01–2:02]. He keeps a "3-month" marker on the chart for this.
- **Weekly/daily:** where he draws support and resistance. He prefers to draw on the weekly "just cuz it's a lot easier" [2:02].
- **4H / 2H:** he zooms in for structure, Fibonacci and entries [3:02, 12:22].
- **Side-by-side charts:** he usually runs the daily, 4H, 2H and sometimes the 15-minute next to each other with mirrored drawings. The 15m is for when he is "trying to really get… a great entry and kind of scale it back in" [30:38].
- He does not let the weekly EMAs drive the decision because the weekly "takes so long". He reads the EMAs on the daily, 4H and 2H [32:38].
- **Routine:** he reviews and redraws every week. He calls the daily check-in the "30 minutes or less" rule: analyse, trade if the setup is there, otherwise set an alert [30:38–31:38, 48:54].

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- **8 EMA and 14 EMA.** Blue line = 8, red line = 14. A blue-over-red cross is bullish. Price trading above the EMAs means "we're heading up", below means "heading down" [13:23]. If he shows only one line, it is the 8 [13:23]. All EMAs pointing down on the daily, 4H and 2H means sells only [31:38–32:38, 42:49].
- **Round numbers ("quarters" / psychological levels).** He uses major whole levels with a mid level ("quarter") between them, for example GBPAUD 1.70 / 1.725 / 1.75 / 1.775 / 1.80, AUDUSD 0.70 / 0.725 / 0.75, and GBPUSD 1.30 / 1.325 / 1.35 / 1.275. He says "170 could mean 169900 it could mean 17200 [1.7200]". In other words, each level is a zone, not an exact price [4:04]. He says he partly moved away from quarters in the Gold Academy and then "went back to it, not like fully" [3:02].
- **Horizontal lines and horizontal rays.** He uses lines for long-term levels and rays to keep the chart uncluttered. He now prefers rays: "I just don't like lines anymore I like rays" [22:32].
- **Labels.** He writes text such as "monthly support 1.70", "weekly mid-level of support 1.725" and "key area of support" [5:07–6:12]. Colours: black or blue for monthly levels, gray or yellow borders for daily levels [24:32].
- **Ellipse tool.** He circles major areas and pattern points instead of drawing long lines [9:17, 10:17].
- **Trendlines** labelled A-B-C-D, and a ray on the key swing low.
- **Fibonacci retracement:** 38.2, 50 and 61.8 [12:22, 17:26].
- **Fibonacci extension:** 1.27 ("27 extension"), 1.618 ("61.8 extension") and 1.80 ("1.80 extension") [12:22–13:23].
- **Long-position (R:R) tool** to measure R:R [12:22–13:23].
- **Heikin Ashi candles** to read the trend without the alternating red/green noise of normal candles [33:39–35:41]. He also checks a line chart for structure [32:38].
- **Chart colours:** purple for up candles and black for down candles. He keeps the charts dark ("black charts") to take emotion out [34:41].
- **Alerts** for when the market reaches the area he expects [31:38, 48:54].
- **Measured distances ("Forex is art"):** he measures how far each recent swing travelled and clones that distance to project the next move. On GBPUSD the drops were repeatedly about 90 pips, so a new drop of about 90 pips becomes a take-profit area [45:52–46:52].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Remove all drawings and indicators and start from a blank chart [1:01, 38:43].
2. **Weekly:** look at only the last ~12 weeks (one quarter) for the overall picture [2:02].
3. Read the current price on the right axis and mark the round-number levels above and below it: major levels (e.g. 1.70, 1.75, 1.80) plus the mid level (e.g. 1.725). Label them monthly, weekly or mid-level support/resistance [3:02–6:12].
4. Look in between those levels and mark where price actually stopped in the past, using wicks and closes. "Sometimes round numbers aren't enough sometimes you got to see exactly where price stopped in the past" [21:31]. He scrolls back through the history to see where price "stopped here, stopped here, stopped here" [8:16–9:17]. If a level breaks, he expects price to run to the next level it has respected [9:17].
5. **Weekly close check:** a wick above a major level that closes back below is a fakeout. "Once you get a close below at a major support or resistance area you know it's going to head all the way back in the opposite direction" [23:32]. He stresses waiting for the weekly candle to close [22:32].
6. **Daily:** read the candles and structure (lower highs and lows vs. higher highs and lows). Look for patterns such as an inverted head and shoulders or a double bottom/top, and draw trendlines [10:17–11:19].
7. **4H/2H:** place the Fibonacci. Find the A-B swing, check where C bounced (38.2/50 retracement) and project the D target with the 1.27, 1.618 and 1.80 extensions [12:22].
8. Check the 8/14 EMAs: the direction of the cross, and whether price is above or below them [13:23].
9. Check Heikin Ashi and/or the line chart to confirm the overall trend [32:38–35:41].
10. Count confluences. He lists "four to five different confluences" on the GBPAUD setup [12:22].
11. Decide a bias (buys only or sells only). Then either enter (step 12) or wait and set an alert [31:38, 48:54].
12. **Entry timing:** wait for a retracement, then take the first, second or third confirming candle in your direction, such as a bullish engulfing or morning star [17:26–18:26].

## 6. Entry rules
- Do not enter while the move still looks like the opposite direction. You need an actual retracement and a bounce. "Could you have gotten in right here no because this is still a sell… you need actual retracement" [12:22].
- After the retracement, enter on the first confirming candles. "That first bullish engulfing or or or Morning Star enter because you have all the confluences you need to get in" [18:26]. He also says "first second or third Candlestick" [17:26].
- On the GBPAUD example the entry is the 4H candle that bounced after the inverted head-and-shoulders, trendline and Fib confluence [12:22].
- **Trend filter:** while the key swing low (the ray on point B) holds, look for buys only [14:24]. When the EMAs point down on the daily, 4H and 2H, "you would only look for sales" [42:49].
- **Re-entry plan on GBPAUD:** wait for a higher low, place a new Fib from A to B, and look for C in the 50–61.8 retracement, where monthly support lines up. Then enter on the first bullish engulfing or morning star [17:26].
- **Don't chase.** If the only possible stop is far away (above the last lower high), don't enter. Wait for a pullback or for a break-and-retest [32:38, 43:50].
- **GBPUSD double bottom:** don't count it as a reversal until price clears the level, comes back down to retest it, and prints a candle you can act on [42:49].
- Order type: not stated. The entries are described as entering on the candle, i.e. a market order at the confirmation candle.

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Put the stop beyond the structure swing: below the higher low or swing low for buys ("stop loss right below here", [12:22]), above the last lower high for sells [32:38].
- He wants a wide stop that gives the trade "room to breathe". A stop that is "so close… gives you no room to breathe" [35:41–36:41].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Fib extensions from the A-B-C swing: 1.27 (about 1:3 on the example), 1.618 (about 1:5) and 1.80 (about 1:7) [13:23].
- The next round-number or "stopped here before" level, e.g. GBPAUD 1.80 [7:15], AUDUSD around 0.7170 [26:34] and GBPUSD 1.275 [42:49].
- The measured-move distance (about 90 pips on GBPUSD) [46:52].
- Swing traders take profit or partial profit at each level along the way [35:41].

## 9. Trade management
- Trail the stop to each new higher low once it forms. Because that low sits above the entry, the stop ends up in profit: "now our stop loss right here can be placed in profit" [16:24].
- Take partial profits at each target level: "take profit take profit take profit or take partial profit take partial profit take partial profit" [35:41].
- Hold through pullbacks and let the trade play out [15:24].
- Use alerts so you don't have to watch the chart [31:38, 48:54].
- **Trendline invalidation rules (GBPAUD):** a break of the trendline alone does not end the trend, especially near support. To invalidate it, price must (1) break the trendline, retest it and continue down to take out the low, and (2) take out the support and the swing low B. "Until it takes B out… it may not be a valid trend line but still a valid Trend" [14:24]. The same idea on AUDUSD and GBPUSD: break the mid level, then take out B, then A [26:34, 44:52].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- R:R on the GBPAUD example: 1:3 at the 1.27 extension, 1:5 at 1.618 and 1:7 at 1.80 [13:23].
- He holds only a few positions and avoids counter-trend trades. "Counter trend trading is very very dangerous" [15:24].
- Risk %, lot sizes and account sizes: not covered in this video.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Only the last ~12 weeks matter on the weekly. "What matters on the weekly is probably the last 12 weeks like the last three months" [1:01–2:02].
- Treat round numbers as zones. "Every time we say 170 it's the area around 170" [4:04].
- Mark where price actually reacted, not just round numbers. "Sometimes round numbers aren't enough sometimes you got to see exactly where price stopped at in the past" [21:31].
- Wait for the weekly candle to close at major levels. "You didn't wait for the candle to close on a weekly time frame" [22:32].
- Keep old drawings on the chart. "Leave your drawing tools on because you never know if it'll come back in a month or two months" [20:30].
- Don't trade counter-trend. "Counter trend trading is very very dangerous" [15:24].
- Look for buys only until the key swing low breaks. "Until it takes this out we are looking for buys only" [14:24].
- When the EMAs are down, sell only. "In that scenario you would only look for sales" [42:49].
- A trendline break alone is not a trend change. "It may not be a valid trend line but still a valid Trend" [14:24].
- Price must be on the right side of the 8 EMA. "Whenever the candlesticks are above it we're heading up" [13:23].
- Wait for a retracement, then enter on the first engulfing or morning star. "That first bullish engulfing or Morning Star enter" [18:26].
- Give trades room. "The one thing you need is market area to breathe" [36:41].
- If there is no setup, set an alert. "Please set an alert before you go" [48:54].
