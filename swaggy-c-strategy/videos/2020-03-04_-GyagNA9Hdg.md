## 1. What this video covers
His main point: traders lose because they enter too early or too late — chasing big candles in "no man's land" leaves no logical stop. He teaches to wait for price to come back to a key level, trend line or Fib area, see a candlestick signal and a higher low/lower high, then scale to the 1-hour/15-minute and enter with a tight stop under the structure, targeting the next major level (often a weekly level with a history of reversals). Examples on USDJPY (110 and 112 levels) and GBPAUD (1.94/1.95/1.9750). He also shows exiting manually when price stalls (dojis + lower high, or no new low). This is the 2020 intraday/swing version.

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)

- Trend lines [2:03, 3:03].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. On weekly/daily, find the major levels and their history (e.g. USDJPY reversed at 112 eight times on the weekly) [7:07].
2. Identify trend and the tools in play: trend line, key level, Fib [3:03, 11:10].
3. If price is far from any level/trend line ("no man's land"), do nothing — wait for price to come back [3:03, 11:10].
4. As price approaches the level/trend line, watch: it will bounce or break [4:04].
5. Look for a candlestick reversal signal (hammer/doji/tweezer) and a higher low (uptrend) or lower high (downtrend) [4:04, 15:14].
6. Scale down (1-hour → 15-minute) and enter on the trigger (break above MA, engulfing, breakout candle) [11:10–12:12, 16:14].
7. Stop below the last low / box; target next major level [5:05].
8. At the target, wait to see break or rejection and look for the next trade [5:05].

## 6. Entry rules
- Only near a level, trend line or Fib area: "if we can get in anywhere else, do not even think about getting in" [11:10].
- Break trades: "get in once it's breaking, after it holds and retests… but get in near that level" [3:03].
- Trend-line bounce: hammer on the trend line → higher low → "most likely wait to cross above moving average" → long [4:04].
- Consolidation at a level: box; doji placed on/above the MA = get in at the close of the doji [6:06–7:07].
- 15-minute trend-line bounce: candle engulfs the prior red candle and breaks above MA → long [11:10–12:12].
- Fib: price stops at the 50% and makes a higher low → enter at the close [14:13].
- Retest of a broken level: after break of 1.95, retest, higher lows on 15m, break above MA → long [15:14–16:14].
- Sell at a major level with multiple confirmations: tweezer top + Marubozu + shooting star + close below MA ("four different confirmations") [18:14].
- Never chase big candles (FOMO) [1:02].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- "If price is going up your stop-loss has to be below the last higher low… if it's going down, above the last lower high" [1:02–2:03].
- Below the consolidation box, a little lower because price wicks out of boxes; "below the last low" [7:07].
- Put stops at a level that, if broken, means the trade is invalid: "if it hits our stop loss it's definitely going in the other direction" [9:09–10:10].
- Too tight is dangerous; give room to breathe, but at the right structure [9:09].
- Bad entries force huge stops that make the trade not worth taking [8:08].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- "Our take profit level is always the next key level or key area" [19:15].
- Use history: USDJPY TP at 112 because it reversed there 8 times on the weekly; don't set TP beyond it [7:07–8:08].
- "Always get out at a major support or resistance level; don't try to hold it unless you see it break through" [12:12].
- TP is an area/zone; anywhere within the grey box is acceptable [12:12].
- Fib -27 extension as TP [14:13].
- R:R examples: 3:1, 5:1, 3:1, 5:1, 5:1, 3:1 (he says "five to one" meaning reward:risk) [5:05, 7:07, 14:13, 16:14, 18:14, 20:15].

## 9. Trade management
- Manual early exit when price stalls: several dojis (he counts 5) at a level then a lower high → "get out, manually take the money" [10:10].
- Exit a short when price prints a hammer, consolidates and doesn't make a lower low — "price is stalling, the trade is done, get out" [19:15–20:15].
- Don't panic-close on normal drawdown; good entries mean little drawdown [13:13].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- Illustration: "if you're risking $100 your reward will be $500; risking $200 you'll be a thousand" [7:07].
- Win rate: "nobody is ever a 100 percent profitable trader… 80/20, 70/30, 90/10" [22:15].
- Weekly P&L framing: lose $1,000, make $8,000 → net $7,000 [22:15].
- Says he's traded five years; first two years losing [1:02].
- No risk % or lot sizes.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts

- No man's land: "when price is way up here in no-man's land you can't get in, you have to be patient" [3:03].
- Stops: "your stop-loss has to be below the last higher low… above the last lower high" [1:02].
- TP: "always get out at a major support or resistance level" [12:12].
- Patience: "price will come back to the level you're looking at" [11:10].
- Retrace: "you have to wait for a retrace, price will always retrace" [17:14].
- Manual exit: "five dojis and a lower high, get out" [10:10].
- You will lose: "you will always lose money in the market no matter what; the goal is to make way more money than you lose" [22:15].
