## 1. What this video covers
The first ~7 minutes are channel/Academy announcements. From [7:07] he plays a ~10-11 minute excerpt of an Academy video recorded "about a month ago" (so roughly May 2020) in which he lays out his intraday/swing "level-to-level" strategy: key level -> trend direction -> candlestick confirmation (e.g. bullish engulfing) with price above the moving averages on the 1H/2H/4H -> drop to the 15m/5m for a break-of-structure entry -> ride to the next key level / mid-level, with Fibonacci (A-B-C + 27 extension) as an extra target tool. He then contrasts it with a scalping version of the same approach (repeated in-and-out entries with the trend, exiting at every consolidation). He states at [4:05]-[5:06] that, even by mid-2020, he no longer trades "structurally and foundationally" exactly this way, but still uses the same principles.

- Time split: scalping is "maybe three percent"; "ninety-seven percent I like swinging into intraday trades" [16:16].
- Timeframes:
  - Trend/structure: 4H (the "overall trend on a four-hour" decides scalp direction [16:16]).
  - Confirmation: 1H, 2H, or even 4H (bullish engulfing + price above MAs) [8:07]; he does the write-up on the 2H [16:16].
  - Entry: 15m (break of structure / higher low) [8:07]; "use the fifteen and five minutes to enter" [10:09]; the written step says "scale down to five or two minutes" [17:17] (likely "five or 15 minute" — wording uncertain [?]). Scalping section says "scale down to the five [or] 30-minute [?]" [18:19].
- Holding: ride a trade from one key level to the next (or at least to the mid-level), described as "250 pips or 500 pips" moves [18:19].

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- Key levels (support/resistance) and mid-levels between them (he expects price to retest "the mid level").
- Moving averages (two MAs; "price goes above the moving averages", "moving average crosses over"). Periods/types not stated in this video.
- Candlestick patterns as confirmation: hammer, morning star, inverted head & shoulders, bullish engulfing, doji + push (morning star) [7:07], [14:15]; marubozu-type full-body candles as momentum [11:11].
- Structure: higher lows/higher highs, "legs" (he draws legs, "not a trend line") [9:07]; consolidation boxes and box breaks; counter-trend lines on lower timeframes [17:17].
- Fibonacci: A-B-C drawn, "drag it straight through price", targets the 27 extension (-0.27) [13:15]-[14:15]; also AB=CD style leg matching ("match to our second leg") [13:15].
- Chart colors: he normally trades on all-black ("black") charts and changes candle colors only so viewers can see: "I'm trying to get you guys to understand black charts but just so you guys can see it better" [11:11].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
His own written summary at [17:17] ("for my strategy, intraday swing but more so intraday trading"), combined with the walkthrough:
1. **Identify key levels.** "First thing you do is see what it does at a key level" [7:07]. Price must be at/near a key level before he analyzes for a trade.
2. **Determine the trend** from those levels/structure (4H) and trade only with it: "we don't counter-trend trade at all" [17:17].
3. **Identify confirmations** on the 1H/2H/4H: a bullish (or bearish) candlestick confirmation such as hammer, morning star, inverted H&S, bullish engulfing, AND price moving above the moving averages [7:07]-[8:07]. For box/consolidation setups, a breakout of the consolidation range and above the MAs [8:07]-[9:07].
4. **Judge candle quality on the breakout**: a full-body, barely-wicked candle breaking the box = real momentum; a wicky candle breaking the box = no confirmation [11:11]-[12:12].
5. **Scale down to the 15m (or 5m)** and wait for a break of structure: a higher low (uptrend) / counter-trend-line break / MAs crossing back up / any lower-timeframe bullish confirmation [8:07], [17:17].
6. **Enter**, stop below the higher low/structure, target the next key level (partials at the mid-level), and use Fib extensions as extra confluence for the target [8:07], [12:12]-[14:15].

## 6. Entry rules
- Must be at a key level first.
- Higher-timeframe (1H/2H/4H) confirmation: bullish engulfing (or similar) + price above the MAs.
- Entry trigger on 15m/5m: "you enter only on a break of structure, which is if you do see a counter trend line like on a five minute time frame, once price breaks the counter trend line or the moving averages cross back up or you get any type of bullish confirmation on a lower time frame, you enter" [17:17].
- On the box-breakout example: enter on the higher lows once price has broken the box: "you're gonna enter any one of these higher lows, especially when it broke the box for sure" [9:07]. Can re-enter each new higher low ("make another higher low, enter") [10:09].
- Only full-momentum breakout candles qualify; a wicky breakout candle is not a valid entry [11:11]-[12:12].
- Order type: market entries implied; not explicitly stated.
- Scalping variant: re-enter every time price breaks out of a small consolidation box in the trend direction [15:16]-[16:16].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- "Stop loss right below here" = below the 15m higher low that triggered the entry [8:07].
- On the full-momentum box breakout: "stop-loss right below here, just low, or even this lower [low] over here" [12:12] — below the most recent low or the lower one beneath it.
- No pip size stated.

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- Target is the next key level: "enter to at least... this next level up here, but mainly up here" [8:07].
- Mid-level between key levels is the minimum / partial target: hold "from one level to the next level or at least to the mid level, for 250 pips or 500 pips" [18:19].
- Fibonacci extension: A-B-C Fib, target "27 extension" (-27%), matches the second-leg projection [13:15]-[14:15].
- At a key level he doesn't assume continuation — "see what it does at this key level" [13:15].

## 9. Trade management
- Partial profits at the mid-level or when approaching the next level: "this is what I'm going to take partial profits... for 250 pips you want to... take some money out" [8:07]; "if you want to take a little bit of profit out now and take partial profit you can" [10:09].
- If structure breaks (price breaks below the last higher low) he expects a retest of the mid-level and exits/takes profit; re-enters later back on the 4H when structure resumes [10:09]-[11:11].
- Scalp variant: exit whenever price consolidates; re-enter on the box break [15:16]-[16:16], [18:19].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Analyze only at key levels: "When price gets to that exact level, then I analyze" [7:07].
- Never counter-trend: "We don't counter trend trade at all" [17:17].
- Need full-candle momentum on a breakout: "This candlestick is full, barely any wick, full momentum" vs "a bunch of wicks... there's no indication" [11:11].
- Use every tool: "I look at everything, look at the candlesticks" [11:11]–[12:12]; also fundamentals [11:11].
- Don't assume continuation at a key level: "Don't just automatically assume price to head up there, see what it does at this key level" [13:15].
- Scalpers must still go with the trend: "If the trend is up, we are looking for buys" [15:16].
- Master one pair: "Focus on mastering one pair as opposed to trading different pairs" [14:15].
- Scalping is costly: "you'll get hit with the same spread... and fees and commissions" [19:21].
