## 1. What this video covers
This is mostly a vlog about losing out on a penthouse, touring apartments and moving. The strategy content is a set of live clips from an Instagram Live session where he trades GBPUSD short and also looks at GBPAUD ("GA") and GBPJPY ("GJ"). He explains the setup in terms of market structure: a lower high, and the 1H market moving in "three or four legs" (leg, retracement, leg, retracement). He uses Fibonacci and a "clone the previous drop" projection for targets. He describes his workflow: mark up on TradingView, execute on MT4. He gives his sizing (1.00 lot, about $300 risk) and his management rules: scale in when the trade is going well, and move the stop above the latest lower high once new structure forms. He also states a rule against trading while upset. This is his 2020 intraday/swing approach. The captions in the trading clips are very garbled, so many details are uncertain (marked [?]).

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
Reconstructed from the GBPUSD clips [3:23-8:27]:
1. Find a **lower high** on GBPUSD and compare it to the previous high ("we have a lower high and compare it to this high right here") [3:23]. This sets a bearish bias.
2. Drop to the **1-hour** chart and count legs. Price moves in three or four legs: "leg one, retracement, leg two, retracement, counter trend". He waits for the next leg down ("waiting for leg [three] to pull it down") [3:23].
3. Decide whether to enter now or wait for price to clear a level first ("I'm gonna figure if I want to wait for it to clear this and go down here, if I want to get in right now") [4:25]. He enters now because he doesn't want to "miss out on too much profit".
4. Compare related GBP pairs (GU, GA, GJ) for the clearest one. Zoom out and use Fibonacci on GA [4:25].
5. Place the stop above the structure high [shown on chart; level only partly heard, see Trade 1].
6. For the target, move to the **4-hour** chart. Clone the previous drop and project it from the current point. Pick TP near where the cloned leg ends (around 1.2200-1.2170 [?]) [8:26].

## 6. Entry rules
- **Setup:** a lower high, then the 1H leg/retracement count, then wait for the next impulsive leg in the trend direction [3:23].
- **Order type:** market execution on MT4 (implied: "I'm definitely getting [in] GU right now") [6:26].
- **Size:** 1.00 lot initially [7:26].
- **Scaling in:** "if sometime midway in the trade I see it's going well, I will adjust that and add ... scale in another trade and move my stop loss to break-even" [7:26].

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- The initial stop goes above the structure (lower high) for a short ("stop-loss right here") [3:23]. The level was heard as roughly 1.2340 (see Trade 1).
- **Moving to break-even or profit:** he does it based on structure, not a fixed pip count. "Once I get like 20, 30 pips in profit — not even 20, 30 pips — more so when the market makes structure, so it makes a lower high and a lower low, I will put it above that lower high" [7:26]. The goal is break-even or a little profit, "so I have a risk-free trade" [7:26].

## 8. Take-profit and targets

## 9. Trade management
- **Scale in** when the trade is going well, and move the stop to break-even at the same time [7:26].
- **Trail by structure:** after a new lower high and lower low, move the stop above that lower high [7:26].
- **Early exit on a reversal (not a retracement):** "if it's retraced I'll leave it in there. If I see an actual reversal and I'm up $5, I'm just gonna take the money" [9:27]. Example amounts are $100 or $500 in profit.
- He accepts the outcome either way: "losses and wins and break even" [6:26] [?].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing

- **Risk about $300** on the trade: "I will risk ... $300" [7:26]. On GBPUSD, 1 lot is about $10/pip, so this means a stop of about 30 pips (inference).
