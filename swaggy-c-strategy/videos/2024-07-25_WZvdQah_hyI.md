## 1. What this video covers
A list of "brutally honest" lessons from his first decade, mostly about discipline, mindset, money goals, and his businesses. The strategy-relevant parts are short. He says small stop losses don't work long term and that he now uses 40-50 pip stops with 185-250 pip targets (1:3-1:4) as a swing trader. He also says you don't need to trade every day, week, or month, and that swing trading is less stressful than day trading (one to three trades every two weeks, held a week or two). He adds that risk management and psychology matter more than technicals. This reflects his 2024 swing-trading version.

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- **Wide stops, 40-50 pips:** "my experience I've seen having stop losses like 40 and 50 pip stop losses give you the breathing room you need because again I am a swing Trader" [6:07].
- **Small stops fail over years:** "small stop losses do not work over a multi-year track record" [4:05]. A small stop "get you out way earlier than you're supposed to"; the market "retrace a little bit seven Pips five Pips stopped you out and then came crashing down your analysis was right" [5:06].
- Stop size only matters relative to target: "all that matters is your risk reward ratio" [6:07]. Example: a 200-pip stop with a 1,000-pip target (preparing for Brexit) "is a good trade that's a one in5" [6:07-7:07].

## 8. Take-profit and targets
- "my take profit is always like 200 Pips 250 225 185 right so it's always a 1 to three to four risk reward ratio" [6:07].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- **R:R:** 1:3 to 1:4 standard [6:07].
- **Risk %:** professionals "keep their risk under a certain percentage" [2:03] (no number given). Bad example: traders "risking 30 40 50%" [28:22].
- **Leverage:** "you have too high of a leverage you risk entirely way too much" listed as a core problem [1:02].
- **Priority:** "someone who has their psychology and risk management down packed ... but has iffy technicals is going to be so much better far off than someone who is a technical Master" [28:22].
- **Account size:** don't try to flip $10/$100 accounts; save "two3 $4,000" for an account or get a prop account [22:17-24:18].
- **Trading breaks:** "every single day is not a trading day every single week is not a trading week and every single month ... is not a trading month" [7:07]. He might "trade for three weeks straight and then ... take two weeks off one week off" [7:07]; or "trade for four months straight and then take two months off" [8:08]. Breaks should be a week, two weeks, or a month, not many months [35:23].
- **Goal-based stopping:** set a goal (e.g. $10,000 in a day); when you reach it, "do not try to trade the next day or day after that like stop celebrate" [30:22-31:22].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Wide stops: "having stop losses like 40 and 50 pip stop losses give you the breathing room you need" [6:07].
- Targets 185-250 pips: "my take profit is always like 200 Pips 250 225 185" [6:07].
- R:R over stop size: "all that matters is your risk reward ratio" [6:07].
- Don't trade every day: "you do not need to trade every single day" [7:07].
- Skip the holidays: "I don't trade near Christmas" [9:09].
- Trust your own analysis over the community: "always use trust your own analysis no matter what" [34:23].
- Stop after hitting a goal: "when you get that do not try to trade the next day" [31:22].
- Don't flip tiny accounts: "there's no chance in the world you are going to flip a $10 account to $10 million" [21:16-22:17].

## 13. Changes from his earlier approach (as he states them)
- **Stops:** "I was somebody who had small stop loss in 2014 2015 I had the 10 pip stop losses the five pip stop losses the I listened to people out there who said if you have more than a 20 pip stop loss you don't know how to trade I did all of that" [6:07]. Now 40-50 pips.
- **Day trading to swing trading:** "I have switched from day trading to swing trading" [14:14]. He admits "you didn't get rich from swing trading you got rich from day trading that's a fact" [24:18]. Day trading suited him when he had "no wife no kids no Mansion no money" and could be on the charts all day; with family and businesses, swing trading the "same exact trade" held a week or two is "so so much less stressful" [24:18-25:20].
- **Future:** he might later stop day-to-day trading and just hold longer-term investments [14:14-15:14]. This foreshadows his 2025 move to position trading.
