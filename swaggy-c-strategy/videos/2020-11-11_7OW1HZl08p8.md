## 1. What this video covers
Recorded at ~3 a.m. LA time, it uses his Sunday GBPUSD call (no entry until London session Tuesday; target 1.32500) and his GBPAUD call to argue that traders lose because they don't focus on 1–2 pairs, lack risk management and psychology. Strategy specifics: wait for the London session entry, trade level to level on the 1H for 70–90 pip moves, a mid-level 1.32500 identified from a weekend gap, needing a bullish candle close above resistance, wick vs close for structure breaks, GBP news timing, pair selection process (start with 7–10 pairs, narrow down), and not over-risking. 2020 level-to-level era.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- Level to level; GU trade described as "not a 200-300 pip swing trade... literally going to be 70, 80 pips" [12:08].
- 1H chart for GU analysis [3:01].

## 3. Markets, pairs and sessions
- Core pairs: gold, GU (GBPUSD), GA (GBPAUD) — unchanged since March/April/June; also AU (AUDUSD) as a favorite ("slow but the structure of AU is beautiful") [5:03], [8:05]. Doesn't trade EURUSD (only analyzed it on IG Live).
- Pair selection: start with 7–10 pairs and narrow to the ones you trade best; not just one pair, since a single pair can consolidate for two weeks [8:05].
- If none of his pairs presents an opportunity in a week, he doesn't trade that week [5:03]-[6:03].
- Session: GU entry expected "about 2 a.m. Eastern time, London session, on Tuesday" [2:01]-[3:01].
- News: GBP news around 4:30 a.m. New York (1:30 a.m. LA, ~9:30 London [?]) nearly every day; "about an hour into that London session the pound is moving" [14:08].
- Fundamentals: election-day USD weakness drove GU's ~130-pip push on Nov 2–3 2020; didn't trade gold the prior week due to election uncertainty [9:05].

## 4. Tools and indicators (with exact settings)
- Key levels and mid-levels; 1.32500 GU mid-level reinforced by a weekend gap (Sept 4 close / Sept 7 open) and prior wicks [11:06].
- Rectangles around levels.
- Candle closes (bullish engulfing / closing above resistance).
- Economic calendar for GBP news [14:08].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
1. Sunday analysis posted to Academy: bias and expected timing of entry (London Tuesday) and target 1.32500 [2:01]-[3:01].
2. Mark key levels and mid-levels using previous price action, gaps and wicks [11:06]-[12:08].
3. Trade from one level to the next; skip moves that are too short [12:08].
4. Require a candle close beyond resistance (bullish engulfing / body close) before expecting the next level [12:08].
5. Consider fundamentals/news timing (GBP news ~1 hour into London) [14:08].

## 6. Entry rules
- Wait for the session he expects the entry in (London) rather than trading the Asian chop: GU "did nothing, it was consolidating the entire time" before 2 a.m. [3:01].
- Close above resistance needed: "if we can get a bullish engulf... and it can close above this major resistance" — then GU "is about to start rocking" [12:08].
- A wick below support is not a valid break: "It did not close below, it appears [?] below and wicked below; that does not constitute as a break of structure" [6:03]-[7:04].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- Don't risk more than you're comfortable losing: "You got a $200 account and you're risking $175, for what?" [15:09]
- Detachment requires proper risk: losing $1,000 on a quarter-million-dollar account "is not that big of a deal" [16:09].
- Stops trading for a while if he loses too much [6:03], [7:04].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Focus on few pairs: "I focus on these three pairs... one or the other will present an opportunity for me this week; if it doesn't, guess what, I'm not trading this week" [5:03]-[6:03].
- Narrow down: "start off with, let's say, seven to ten pairs and break them down to the pairs that... you trade the best in" [8:05].
- Wick isn't a break: "It did not close below... that does not constitute as a break of structure" [6:03].
- Level to level: "Just go from one level to the next" [12:08].
- Risk: "This is why you don't risk more than you're comfortable with losing" [15:09].
- Full-circle trading: technicals without "risk management... psychology" fails [4:02], [13:08].
