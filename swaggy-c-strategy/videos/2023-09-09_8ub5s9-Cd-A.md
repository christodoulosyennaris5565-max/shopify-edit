## 1. What this video covers
This is an Instagram Q&A. Most of it is about motivation, his life story, jobs and promoting his course. The strategy content covers his favourite trading days (Monday-Wednesday) and the Asian-into-London session on gold, GBPAUD and AUDUSD. He gives a one-line summary of his strategy: price action, news via TradingView, one moving average, psychological/key levels and trend lines. He also explains how to scale a small account by slowly raising lot size, why entries go against people (late entries, too few confluences, tight stops), that he swing trades with about 20 pips of drawdown against a 140-pip target, and that his pre-trade checklist is 1:3 (or 1:2) R:R plus 2-3 confluences. It reflects his 2023 swing approach.

## 2. Trading style, holding period and timeframes
- "I'm a swing trader, and I used to be a day trader obviously, but I'm a swing trader, which means I hold my trade a little bit longer" [10:02].
- He can be "okay with the market going against me 20 pips because I'm aiming for 140" [10:02].
- No chart timeframes are mentioned.

## 3. Markets, pairs and sessions
- Best days: "believe it or not it is actually Monday through Wednesday" [2:01]. He rejects "don't trade Mondays". The market finds its footing on Sunday, and "the market moves crazy on Monday".
- Pairs: gold (XAUUSD), GBPAUD, AUDUSD. AUDUSD is "a little slower at times" [2:01].
- Session: "Monday night, that Asian session going into London session, that is a very very volatile session and that is the main session" [2:01]. He has traded those pairs and that session since 2019.
- Why not later in the week: by Monday-Wednesday the "midweek reversal" hasn't happened yet. Thursday night into Friday brings news (e.g. the unemployment rate) and falling volatility as the work week ends. Wednesday has FOMC and "good fundamental announcements", but "technical wise Monday through Wednesday is going to be your best friend" [2:01-3:01].

## 5. Analysis process, step by step
The video gives no full process. Pieces he mentions:
1. Read the TradingView news feed during the week (about an hour, Mon-Wed) to check sentiment and fundamentals [3:01].
2. Mark the technical picture: psychological/key levels, trend lines, one moving average [4:01].
3. Before taking a trade, check it against the trading plan. Is it 1:3 R:R (or 1:2, "whatever your trading plan is")? "Do I have two or three confluences?" [15:07].

## 6. Entry rules
- Enter after 2-3 confluences, not late: losing traders "got in [after] four confluences, too late, and missed the entire move ... the market done went up 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 pips and you got in at the 50 pips not realizing the market has to correct itself" [10:02].
- Don't chase. In his gold example (1897 → 1917) he did not enter. "The old me would have tried to get in at 1913 ... you done got in 170 pips too late" [14:05].
- Order type is not discussed.

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- Leave room for drawdown. He accepts about 20 pips against him on a trade aimed at 140 pips [10:02]. Exact stop sizes are not given.
- "Can you be in drawdown for an hour before the market is positive all the way in your favor? I don't think some of you guys can" [10:02].

## 10. Risk management and position sizing
- **Small-account scaling** [6:02-7:02]: "you can't really get into big trades with a small account unless you slowly scale that account and slowly raise the lot size every now and then". Example:
  - $300 account, 0.04 lots, makes about $150 over a few days → $450.
  - Going from $450 toward $700: move up to 0.05, maybe 0.06 or 0.07.
  - Don't use a standard lot on a small account: "all it takes is 40 pips and your account is blown" [6:02]. The market can move 20-40 pips in minutes on news.
- If you lose while following your plan, accept it. If you keep losing while following the plan, "it may be time to get a whole new plan" [15:07].
