## 5. Analysis process, step by step
This is reconstructed from the gold walkthrough [25:21-29:27]:
1. Price reaches a level he has marked ("once it got right here") [shown on chart, not stated].
2. At that level, a large **marubozu confirmation candle** prints in the direction of the trade (up).
3. Price pushes higher, comes back down and **consolidates**.
4. Price pulls back and **bounces off the moving averages**.
5. He drops to a **smaller timeframe** and enters once he sees the bounce actually happen.
6. After entry, price consolidates again ("went up, went down, a little bit of a doji"), then breaks higher.
7. He sets a target at a round number ($2,000 per ounce) and watches candle closes and wicks to decide whether to exit early.
8. For bigger-picture direction he checks the macro view: a weak dollar or DXY means gold keeps rising.

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Use moving-average bounces as entries. MAs are dynamic S/R: "Moving averages act as a moving, you know, support and resistance." [26:22]
- Wait to see the bounce before entering: "I actually got in right here once I saw the actual moving average bounce." [25:21]
- Use a marubozu at the level as confirmation: "it made this huge, you know, confirmation marubozu candlestick" [25:21]
- Watch where the candle closes relative to its range: "This is where you got to focus in on the candlestick." [28:27]
- A candle that spikes up and closes near its low means get out: "You should know if price ever does that, it's about to crash all the way down." [28:27]
- A long wick points the other way: "Once you see like a long long long specific wick, understand the other way. Like they didn't just create that long wick for no reason." [28:27]
- Don't stick to a fixed target when the candles say exit. He closed 130 pips short of 2000 [27:24-28:27].
- Gold vs the dollar: "gold is going to keep going up because it's negative correlated with the DXY regardless." [29:28]
- Only trade with full focus: "when your mind isn't there, it's hard to really trade" [17:13]. This is why he cut back on trading during this period.
