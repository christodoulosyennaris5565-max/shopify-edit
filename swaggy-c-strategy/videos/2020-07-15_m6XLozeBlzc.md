## 1. What this video covers
This is mostly a lifestyle vlog: a mentoring call with Eric Thomas, a prayer, his brother and best friend visiting, a diamond chain, and a Swag Academy relaunch announcement. The only strategy content is his answer to an emailed question: "If the market is going in your favor and you're so scared you take the money out, but it keeps going your way, should you get back in?" His answer sets out his 2020 trade-management rules. He does not take full profit early. He moves the stop to break-even or a little profit, but only after market structure forms in his favour. He then trails the stop behind the last higher low (in an uptrend) or lower high (in a downtrend). When he is up about $5,000-10,000 he banks 25-50% as partial profit and lets the rest run risk-free.

## 7. Stop-loss rules
- **No break-even straight after entry:** "you don't want to get in the trade and immediately move your stop loss to break even." [18:22]
- **Move the stop only when structure has formed in your favour:** "When the market structure moves in your direction, then you move your stop loss." [18:22]
- **Where to move it:** to "at least break even, if not, you know, in profit, depending on how, you know, market structure is" [17:21].
- **Trailing by structure:** in a downtrend, move the stop above the last lower high. In an uptrend, move it below the last higher low: "in an uptrend, the last higher low, I believe. Yeah, the last higher low. So, just move it with structure." [17:21]. In the captions he says "move it from at the stop loss to and take profit above the last lower high". This is read as "move the stop loss to profit, above the last lower high" for shorts.

## 9. Trade management
1. Enter the trade with the initial stop. Do not move it straight away [18:22].
2. Wait for price to form new structure in your favour: a new higher low in an uptrend, or a new lower high in a downtrend.
3. Move the stop to break-even or a little profit, behind that structure. The trade is now "risk-free": "the worst case scenario, I got out the trade the same way I got in with no money or an extra hundred." [18:22]
4. Keep trailing the stop behind each new higher low or lower high ("just move it with structure") [17:21].
5. **Partials:** "if I'm up 5,000 or 10,000, I will like take partial profit. I will take 25%, 40%, 50% of that profit" and move it to his bank account [18:22].
6. Let the rest run with its stop at break-even or small profit: "I would let it run no matter what." That gives "the best of both worlds" [18:22].
- **If you already closed early:** "all profit is good profit" (advice from his friend Shai). Don't beat yourself up about closing too early; it comes with experience. Next time, "trust your analysis" and move the stop instead of closing [16:20-17:21].

## 12. Rules, do's and don'ts
- Don't regret taking profit: "understand all profit is good profit." [16:20]
- Don't close the whole trade out of fear. Protect it instead: "I wouldn't recommend taking it all out. I would recommend putting your stop loss at at least break even, if not, you know, in profit" [17:21]
- Don't move the stop to break-even straight after entry: "you don't want to get in the trade and immediately move your stop loss to break even. You don't want to do that." [18:22]
- Move the stop only after structure forms: "When the market structure moves in your direction, then you move your stop loss." [18:22]
- Trail behind the last higher low or lower high: "the last higher low. So, just move it with structure." [17:21]
- Take partials of 25-50% when well in profit: "I will take 25%, 40%, 50% of that profit to bank that into my account" [18:22]
- Let the rest run risk-free: "the other 50% now had its stop loss at break even or a little bit of profit" [18:22]
- Trust your analysis next time: "Now, you know in the future, trust your analysis." [17:21]
